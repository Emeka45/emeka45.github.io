Bayelsa APC Expresses Confidence in Grassroots Strength Ahead of 2027 General Elections

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has stated that its grassroots structures are robust enough for the upcoming 2027 elections, dismissing opposing coalitions and backing President Bola Tinubu.

The All Progressives Congress (APC) in Bayelsa State has expressed strong confidence in the robustness of its grassroots structures as preparations ramp up for the 2027 general elections. The state leadership has firmly dismissed the political significance of emerging opposing political arrangements while throwing its weight behind President Bola Tinubu's administration.

Grassroots Strength and Local Organization

Reacting to recent political realignments, Bayelsa State APC Chairman Warman Weri Ogoriba stated in a weekend statement that the party's membership and network remain fully equipped to prosecute successful election campaigns. Ogoriba emphasized that the ruling party has successfully established solid structures spanning all eight local government areas and political wards across the state.

According to the state chairman, the party's immediate priority is to concentrate on internal organization, disciplined operations, and aggressive voter mobilization. Rather than being distracted by external coalitions, the leadership intends to continue engaging key stakeholders and expanding its membership base throughout local communities. "The task before the party is to remain organised, disciplined and committed to the collective objective of advancing the APC and supporting its candidates at all levels," Ogoriba noted.

Response to the Rainbow Coalition and Wike

The reaffirmation of party strength comes amid shifting political dynamics, notably the emergence of the cross-party political alliance known as the “Rainbow Coalition.” The coalition is associated with Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike, who previously claimed to possess the necessary political machinery to deliver both Rivers and Bayelsa states for President Tinubu in the upcoming 2027 polls as reported by Punch Nigeria and detailed further by The Reporter.

Despite these external political maneuvers, the Bayelsa APC leadership maintains that its existing network of members, stakeholders, and community grassroots networks is more than sufficient. Ogoriba assured party faithful that this internal framework serves as a reliable foundation for the 2027 campaigns.

Next Steps and Leadership Commitment

In his address to party supporters, Ogoriba called for unity and unwavering commitment across all chapters in the state. He assured members that the state working committee will sustain ongoing community outreach, stakeholder consultations, and localized mobilization drives.

Concluding his remarks, the state chairman officially reaffirmed the absolute loyalty of the Bayelsa APC leadership to President Bola Tinubu, confirming that the chapter remains fully dedicated to backing Tinubu and all party candidates in the 2027 general elections.
