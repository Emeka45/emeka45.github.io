Bayelsa APC Expresses Confidence in Grassroots Strength Ahead of 2027 General Elections

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has stated that its grassroots structures are robust enough for the upcoming 2027 elections, dismissing opposing coalitions and backing President Bola Tinubu.

As preparations and political realignments gradually take shape ahead of the upcoming electoral cycle, the All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has publicly expressed strong confidence in the depth and reach of its grassroots network. According to reports detailed by Punch Nigeria, party leadership maintains that its existing structures across the state provide a solid foundation for future electoral contests.

Further elaborating on the political landscape within the state, additional reports from The Reporter indicate that the Bayelsa APC has formally dismissed the relevance of the Rainbow Coalition. The party maintains that its established local framework is more than sufficient to support President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates when the nation heads to the polls in 2027.

While political stakeholders continue to gauge the shifting dynamics in the region, the official stance of the ruling party highlights an ongoing battle for grassroots mobilization and structural dominance in Bayelsa State as the next general elections draw closer.
