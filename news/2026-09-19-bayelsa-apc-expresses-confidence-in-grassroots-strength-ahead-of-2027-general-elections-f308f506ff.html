Bayelsa APC Expresses Confidence in Grassroots Strength Ahead of 2027 General Elections

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has stated that its grassroots structures are robust enough for the upcoming 2027 elections, dismissing opposing coalitions and backing President Bola Tinubu.

The All Progressives Congress (APC) in Bayelsa State has expressed strong confidence in its grassroots network ahead of the 2027 general elections, asserting that its internal structures are robust enough to prosecute successful campaigns without relying on external political alliances. The position of the state chapter was made public following discussions surrounding emerging political realignments and statements from key political actors concerning the upcoming electoral cycle.

Party Structures Across Local Governments

According to the Bayelsa State APC Chairman, Warman Weri Ogoriba, the ruling party has established functional structures spanning all eight local government areas and political wards across the state. In a statement issued on Saturday, Ogoriba emphasized that the combination of the party's formal organization, committed members, and community networks provides a solid foundation for its electoral ambitions. Detailed insights on these developments can be found in reports by Punch Nigeria and The Reporter.

Ogoriba noted that the party remains focused on internal organization, discipline, and expanding its membership base rather than being distracted by external coalitions. He stated:

“The task before the party is to remain organised, disciplined and committed to the collective objective of advancing the APC and supporting its candidates at all levels. We are confident that the combination of the party’s existing structures, committed members, stakeholders and grassroots networks would provide the foundation for a strong electoral campaign in 2027.”

Response to Coalitions and Re-election Support

The reaffirmation of grassroots strength comes amid ongoing political developments, including the emergence of the “Rainbow Coalition”—a cross-party political arrangement linked to Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike. Wike had previously claimed to possess the necessary political structures to deliver Rivers and Bayelsa states for President Bola Tinubu in 2027. Despite these external realignments, the Bayelsa APC leadership chose to dismiss the necessity of such coalitions for its local mobilization.

Concluding his remarks, Ogoriba urged party members and supporters to stay united. He reaffirmed the state leadership's absolute commitment to backing President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates, pledging that the hierarchy will maintain continuous community engagement and stakeholder consultations leading up to the 2027 general polls.
