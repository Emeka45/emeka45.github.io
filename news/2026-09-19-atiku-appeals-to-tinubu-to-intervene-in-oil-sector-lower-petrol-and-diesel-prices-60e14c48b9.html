Atiku Appeals to Tinubu to Intervene in Oil Sector, Lower Petrol and Diesel Prices

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has called on President Bola Tinubu to adopt his policy proposals for lowering petrol and diesel pump prices to alleviate severe economic hardships facing Nigerians.

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has urged President Bola Tinubu to urgently intervene in the nation's oil and gas sector by adopting proposals designed to reduce pump prices for petrol and diesel. According to THISDAYLIVE, the appeal aims to ease the heavy cost of living burden currently weighing down citizens across the country.

As detailed by The Reporter, Atiku's intervention centers on specific market adjustments that he argues can relieve economic pressure on everyday Nigerians. The rising cost of petroleum products has been a central driver of inflation and general hardship, prompting ongoing debates over market regulation and state intervention.

The administration of President Tinubu has not yet formally responded to the latest policy recommendations put forward by the former vice president. Meanwhile, the public remains focused on potential measures the federal government might pursue to stabilize fuel prices and mitigate economic strains.
