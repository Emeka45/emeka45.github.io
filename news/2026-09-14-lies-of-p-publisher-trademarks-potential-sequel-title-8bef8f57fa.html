Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Potential Sequel Title

Recent trademark filings suggest the next installment in the Lies of P franchise may adapt a different classic literary universe.

Publisher Neowiz has sparked speculation regarding the next chapter in its dark fairytale universe following recent trademark filings. According to reports from Eurogamer, filings at the European Union Intellectual Property Office point to a project titled 'Wonders of O'. Analysis of these filings by Polygon indicates the title heavily hints at the direction of the upcoming Soulslike sequel, shifting its source material focus away from Pinocchio. While the publisher has not officially detailed the project, the trademark registration strongly suggests development is underway for the follow-up.
