Federal Government Suspends 20 NSCDC Officers as NBA Investigates Deaths of 37 Miners in Niger State

The Federal Government has suspended 20 Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps officers, including the Niger State Commandant, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners in custody. Concurrently, the Nigerian Bar Association has established an investigative team to probe the incident.

The Federal Government has suspended 20 officers of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), including the Niger State Commandant, Suberu Aniviye, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners in the Corps’ custody in Minna, Niger State. The incident occurred on Thursday, September 17, 2026. The Federal Government suspension of 20 NSCDC officers came alongside the formation of a 10-member independent investigative committee announced by the Minister of Interior, Olubunmi Tunji Ojo.

Government Panel and Mandate

The action follows a directive from President Bola Tinubu for a comprehensive investigation into the deaths in custody. The newly established committee is chaired by retired Department of State Services Deputy Director General Jonathan Kure, with former Director General of the Nigerian Law School Prof. Isa Hayatu Chiroma serving as secretary. Other members include retired AIG Hosea Hassan Karma, Prof. Olayinka Buhari, representatives of the Minna Emirate Council and the Niger State Government, Liman Sulaiman representing the Miners Association of Nigeria, lawyer and human rights activist Deji Adeyanju, Blueprint journalist Zainab Suleiman Okino, and public affairs analyst George Agbakahi.

The panel has been tasked with establishing the identities of the deceased, investigating the circumstances surrounding their arrest and detention, and determining the direct causes of death. Furthermore, the committee is mandated to examine issues of responsibility, complicity, negligence, and misconduct, while recommending appropriate disciplinary actions, compensation where applicable, and preventive safeguards. The panel has been given a two-week window to complete its assignment and submit its report.

Scope of Suspensions and Safeguards

The suspended personnel include the Niger State Commandant as well as 20 other officers involved in station guard, arrest, investigation, intelligence, legal, and guard duties. Minister Tunji Ojo instructed all relevant officers and NSCDC leadership to cooperate fully with the investigation and to preserve all records and material evidence, warning that any attempt to destroy evidence, intimidate witnesses, or obstruct the probe would be dealt with severely.

Nigerian Bar Association Investigation

Concurrently, the Nigerian Bar Association (NBA) has named a team to probe the death of 37 miners in Niger. Headed by Senior Advocate of Nigeria and former NBA General Secretary Jonathan Gunu Taidi, the independent team was established under NBA President Oyinkansola Badejo-Okusanya. The association stated that the deaths of 37 individuals while in state custody demand utmost scrutiny, emphasizing the need for transparent forensic and medical evaluations, proper ventilation and facility capacity assessments, and the protection of surviving detainees and material witnesses.

The NBA also highlighted broader issues regarding the commercial exploitation of mineral resources, urging authorities to investigate the ownership and financing behind the illegal mining operations rather than focusing solely on labourers. Additionally, the association raised concerns regarding reports that children might have been among those detained and affected, calling for official confirmation of the ages and identities of all involved.
