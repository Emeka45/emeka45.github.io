Tinubu to Broadcast on 66th Independence Anniversary as Shettima Leads Nigeria to UNGA

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key 66th Independence Anniversary, while also mandating Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the country's delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly in New York.

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the country's 66th Independence Anniversary, while Vice President Kashim Shettima has been mandated to lead Nigeria's delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York.

Low-Key Independence Anniversary and National Broadcast

As the Federal Government prepares to mark Nigeria's 66th Independence Anniversary, an inter-ministerial committee has been inaugurated to coordinate celebrations. The committee is chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume, and was represented at its inaugural meeting by Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office.

Akume stated that the anniversary offers a moment for reflection on the legacies and sacrifices of Nigeria's founding figures who fought against colonial rule. He also noted that the celebrations will highlight the administration's reform efforts and achievements amid current socio-economic challenges. Due to time constraints, the SGF directed that the events be kept low-key.

The schedule of events leading up to the national address includes:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma'at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: President Tinubu's nationwide broadcast

The planning committee brings together top government figures, including the Ministers of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Information and National Orientation, Interior, Foreign Affairs, Federal Capital Territory, and Budget and Economic Planning, alongside the National Security Adviser and presidential advisers. Security and law enforcement heads, such as the Inspector General of Police, the Director General of the Department of State Services, and the Commander of the Guards Brigade, are also members.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In international engagements, President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, United States. This development concludes weeks of speculation regarding whether the president would attend the annual gathering in person, following his departure from Abuja on August 30 for a three-week European vacation.

According to a statement issued by Stanley Nkwocha, Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Communications in the Office of the Vice President, Shettima will head a high-level delegation that includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials.

The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme, “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.” During the event, Vice President Shettima will deliver Nigeria's national statement, addressing national priorities on global issues, peace and security, economic growth, and international cooperation.

Shettima's itinerary also features high-level meetings on climate action, the Right to Development, and bilateral talks with foreign leaders, international organizations, and development partners. These engagements aim to advance economic diplomacy, strengthen multilateral cooperation, and advocate for reforms in the global financial architecture. Further details can be found in the reports by The Reporter and Punch Newspapers. Shettima is scheduled to return to Nigeria upon concluding his engagements in New York.
