Lagos State Orders Immediate Relocation of Residents in Flood-Prone Areas Following NiHSA Alert

Following a fresh seven-day flood alert issued by the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency, the Lagos State Government has instructed residents living in low-lying and flood-prone communities to evacuate immediately.

The Lagos State Government has issued a direct directive instructing residents living in low-lying and flood-prone communities across the state to relocate immediately. This urgent safety measure comes on the heels of a fresh seven-day flood alert issued by the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (Punch Nigeria), warning of potential heavy flooding risks within the specified period.

Authorities have emphasized that timely compliance with the relocation order is critical to safeguarding lives and minimizing property destruction as vulnerable areas face heightened environmental threats. State officials continue to monitor water levels and weather patterns closely in response to the warning relayed by TheCable.

Residents in the affected zones are advised to move to higher ground temporarily while emergency management agencies maintain heightened surveillance and preparedness across the state.
