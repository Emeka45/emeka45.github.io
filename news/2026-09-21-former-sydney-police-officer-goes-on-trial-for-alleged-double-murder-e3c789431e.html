Former Sydney Police Officer Goes on Trial for Alleged Double Murder

The trial of Beaumont Lamarre-Condon, a former New South Wales police officer accused of murdering his ex-partner and his new boyfriend, has begun in Sydney. The defendant has pleaded not guilty.

The trial of Beaumont Lamarre-Condon, a former New South Wales police officer accused of murdering his ex-partner and his new boyfriend, has begun in Sydney. Detailed accounts of the case were reported by the BBC News and The Guardian. The defendant has pleaded not guilty to two charges of domestic violence-related murder and one of aggravated break and enter.

Trial Proceedings and Prosecution Opening

The trial is expected to run for three months before Justice Natalie Adams. Prior to opening statements, a 15-member jury was selected, featuring three extra people as contingencies for illness and unforeseen circumstances, exceeding the typical 12-member standard.

During his opening address, prosecutor Brett Hatfield SC detailed that Lamarre-Condon was driven by an "obsessive and jealous infatuation" with 26-year-old TV presenter Jesse Baird after a brief romantic relationship. The prosecution stated that this infatuation manifested in compulsive behaviors that escalated after Baird ended all contact in November 2023. According to the court, the accused used a friend's phone to falsely claim he had cancer, stalked his ex-lover, and tampered with his car.

The prosecution also noted that Lamarre-Condon had earlier offered to plead guilty to a single charge of manslaughter relating to Baird's death, but the offer was rejected.

Allegations Surrounding the Killings

Prosecutors allege that on the day of the killings, Lamarre-Condon set his alarm for 04:30 local time, switched to a burner phone, picked up a rental car, and waited outside Baird's home in the Sydney suburb of Paddington for three hours disguised as an Uber Eats driver. He allegedly carried a surfboard bag that he had previously purchased to dispose of Baird's body.

Using a key he had previously obtained, Lamarre-Condon allegedly entered the home and shot Baird in the kitchen. He then allegedly killed Baird's new partner, 29-year-old Qantas flight attendant Luke Davies, in the bedroom as Davies attempted to call emergency services. Hatfield told the court that the accused planned Baird's murder and eliminated Davies to remove him as a witness.

Cover-Up and Investigation

In the aftermath of the alleged killings, the defendant reportedly tried to cover up the crimes by sending messages from Baird's mobile phone. The couple's bodies were discovered eight days later hidden in surfboard bags in a rural area 200 kilometers away.

Police initially became aware of the case after bloodied items belonging to Baird and Davies were found in a bin in Sydney's south. Lamarre-Condon subsequently handed himself in at a police station and was charged, leading to his dismissal from the New South Wales Police force weeks later.

The separate aggravated break and enter charge stems from an alleged August 2023 incident in which Lamarre-Condon allegedly broke into Baird's home and stole his phone and wallet.
