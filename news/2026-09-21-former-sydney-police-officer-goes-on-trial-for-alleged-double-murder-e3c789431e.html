Former Sydney Police Officer Goes on Trial for Alleged Double Murder

The trial of Beaumont Lamarre-Condon, a former New South Wales police officer accused of murdering his ex-partner and his new boyfriend, has begun in Sydney. The defendant has pleaded not guilty.

The trial of Beaumont Lamarre-Condon, a 31-year-old former police officer in New South Wales, commenced in the Supreme Court in Sydney as prosecutors outlined allegations of stalking, obsession, and the misuse of police authority prior to a double murder The Guardian. Lamarre-Condon has formally pleaded not guilty to two counts of murder and one charge of aggravated break and enter BBC.

Court Details and Allegations

During the opening of the trial, the court was told that the former officer allegedly disguised himself as an Uber Eats delivery rider prior to the killings BBC. Further allegations detailed in court claimed that the defendant utilized his position as a law enforcement officer to improperly access CCTV footage and fabricated a cancer diagnosis as part of his interactions surrounding the victims The Guardian. The high-profile legal proceedings continue in the New South Wales Supreme Court as the panel of judges and legal teams examine the evidence presented by the prosecution.
