Bayelsa APC Expresses Confidence in Grassroots Structures Ahead of 2027 Elections

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has declared confidence in its existing grassroots network, dismissing the Rainbow Coalition and affirming its backing for national leadership heading into the 2027 general elections.

The All Progressives Congress (APC) in Bayelsa State has expressed strong confidence in its existing grassroots structures and membership network, asserting that the party is fully equipped to execute its campaign strategies ahead of the 2027 general elections. The declaration by the state leadership comes amidst evolving political realignments in the region, including the emergence of the Rainbow Coalition and claims regarding political structures capable of delivering outcomes for President Bola Tinubu.

Leadership Stance on Grassroots Strength

Reacting to recent political developments, Bayelsa State APC Chairman Warman Weri Ogoriba released a statement affirming that the party's institutional framework remains robust. According to the state leadership, the APC has established formal structures spanning all eight local government areas and political wards within Bayelsa State. Ogoriba emphasized that rather than relying on external political alliances, the party's primary objective is to concentrate on internal organization, membership expansion, and voter mobilization.

Detailing the party's roadmap, Ogoriba noted that the combination of established local structures, committed members, key stakeholders, and community grassroots networks will form the bedrock of the party's electoral campaign. "The task before the party is to remain organised, disciplined and committed to the collective objective of advancing the APC and supporting its candidates at all levels," Ogoriba stated, as detailed in reports by The Punch.

Context of Political Realignments

The reaffirmation of strength by the Bayelsa APC follows recent political maneuvering in the region, notably the formation of the Rainbow Coalition—a cross-country political arrangement linked to Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike. The coalition and associated claims have centered on building unified political architectures to secure electoral support for President Bola Tinubu's 2027 re-election bid across states like Rivers and Bayelsa.

Despite these external developments, the Bayelsa APC leadership has opted to anchor its strategy on its indigenous grassroots framework. Further details regarding the party's stance can be found in coverage by The Reporter.

Next Steps and Mobilization

Looking forward, the Bayelsa APC has called on its support base and members across communities to remain united and focused. The state leadership has committed to sustaining active stakeholder consultations and grassroots outreach to solidify support. Additionally, the party reaffirmed its unwavering backing for President Bola Tinubu and all other party candidates as preparations for the 2027 general elections continue to gather momentum across the state.
