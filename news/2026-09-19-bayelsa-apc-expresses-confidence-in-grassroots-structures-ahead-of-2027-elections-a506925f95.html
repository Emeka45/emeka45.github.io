Bayelsa APC Expresses Confidence in Grassroots Structures Ahead of 2027 Elections

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has declared confidence in its existing grassroots network, dismissing the Rainbow Coalition and affirming its backing for national leadership heading into the 2027 general elections.

The All Progressives Congress (APC) in Bayelsa State has reiterated its faith in its existing party architecture, asserting that its established grassroots network and structures are fully sufficient to deliver victory for President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates in the upcoming 2027 general elections. According to reporting from The Punch, the state chapter has firmly anchored its strategic outlook on its local mobilization capacity.

Further elaborating on the political landscape in the state, reports from The Reporter indicate that the party specifically dismissed the newly formed Rainbow Coalition. The chapter maintained that external political alignments or coalitions are unnecessary given the robust framework already operational within the state APC fold.

As political actors across the country begin positioning themselves well in advance of the 2027 polls, the stance taken by the Bayelsa APC highlights the ongoing efforts by state chapters to consolidate internal cohesion and signal organizational readiness to the national leadership.
