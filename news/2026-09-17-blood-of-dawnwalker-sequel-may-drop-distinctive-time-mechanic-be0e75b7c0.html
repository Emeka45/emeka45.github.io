Blood of Dawnwalker Sequel May Drop Distinctive Time Mechanic

Game director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz reveals that the controversial time mechanic featured in the upcoming vampire RPG Blood of Dawnwalker might not return in a potential sequel.

According to Polygon, the developers of the upcoming role-playing game Blood of Dawnwalker are already considering changes for future installments. Game director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz spoke with Eurogamer to discuss the title's distinctive time-cost system, noting that the divisive feature may not necessarily be included in a follow-up project.
