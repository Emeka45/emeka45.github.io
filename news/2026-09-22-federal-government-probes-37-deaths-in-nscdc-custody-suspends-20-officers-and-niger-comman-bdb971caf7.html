Federal Government Probes 37 Deaths in NSCDC Custody, Suspends 20 Officers and Niger Commandant

The Federal Government has launched an independent panel and suspended 20 Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps officers, including the Niger State Commandant, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners in custody.

The Federal Government of Nigeria has initiated an independent investigative panel and suspended 20 personnel from the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), alongside the Niger State Commandant, following the tragic deaths of 37 individuals held in custody as reported by The Reporter. The victims were described as suspected illegal miners.

Authorities have assured the public that there will be no attempt to cover up the circumstances surrounding the fatalities. The inauguration of the independent probe committee is aimed at uncovering how the 37 detainees lost their lives while under the supervision of the security agency according to details provided by The Punch.

The administrative actions, which include the immediate suspension of the state commandant and 19 other officers, represent a decisive response to accountability concerns within the nation's security architecture. As the independent panel commences its inquiry, further developments regarding the disciplinary measures and the official findings of the investigation remain anticipated.
