APC Demands Clarity on Atiku's Petrol Subsidy Proposal as Cost of Living Debate Continues

The All Progressives Congress has challenged Atiku Abubakar to explain the legal and fiscal framework of his proposed petrol production subsidy, following Atiku's call for President Bola Tinubu to adopt measures to lower pump prices.

The political discourse surrounding Nigeria's energy sector and cost of living has intensified as the All Progressives Congress (APC) challenges former Vice President Atiku Abubakar to provide a detailed explanation of his proposed production subsidy for locally refined petroleum products (Punch Newspapers).

The exchange follows recent interventions by Atiku Abubakar, who urged President Bola Tinubu to adopt his specific policy proposals aimed at intervening in the market to reduce petrol prices and alleviate the heavy economic burden currently facing citizens (The Reporter). Atiku's proposal centers on utilizing a production subsidy model designed to bring down the pump price of fuel and ease soaring inflation.

APC Demands Fiscal and Legal Transparency

In response to the proposal, the ruling party questioned the practical implementation of Atiku's plan. The APC specifically asked the former presidential candidate to clearly outline the legal and fiscal basis that would support such a production subsidy mechanism in Nigeria's current economic climate (Punch Newspapers).

The debate highlights ongoing policy disagreements between the administration and opposition figures regarding how best to manage petroleum pricing, energy security, and national economic reforms following the removal of the general fuel subsidy.
