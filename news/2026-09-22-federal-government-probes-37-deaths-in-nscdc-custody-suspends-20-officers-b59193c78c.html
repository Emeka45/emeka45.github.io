Federal Government Probes 37 Deaths in NSCDC Custody, Suspends 20 Officers

The Federal Government has inaugurated an independent probe and suspended 20 Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps officers, including the Niger State Commandant, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners in custody.

The Federal Government has taken decisive action following the tragic deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners while in the custody of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) in Niger State. Authorities have launched an independent investigative committee and vowed that there will be no cover-up in uncovering the circumstances surrounding the fatalities, as reported by Punch Nigeria.

As part of the immediate administrative measures, the government announced the suspension of 20 NSCDC officers alongside the Niger State Commandant. Details of these disciplinary steps were further highlighted by The Reporter, confirming the establishment of a formal panel to investigate the incident thoroughly.

The inquiry aims to determine accountability for the massive loss of life within a custodial facility and address operational conduct concerning detained suspects. Further updates are expected as the newly inaugurated investigative panel commences its work and reviews the events leading up to the deaths in Niger State.
