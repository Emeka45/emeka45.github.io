Federal Government Suspends NSCDC Officers Over 37 Custody Deaths as Falana Demands Prosecution

The Federal Government has suspended 20 Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps officers, including the Niger State Commandant, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners in custody. Meanwhile, human rights lawyer Femi Falana has called for the swift prosecution of those responsible.

The Federal Government has taken decisive administrative action by suspending 20 officers of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), alongside the Niger State Commandant, following a tragic incident that resulted in the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners while in custody in Minna. The disciplinary measures come alongside the establishment of an investigative panel to unravel the circumstances surrounding the fatal incident, as reported by The Reporter.

The mass deaths have sparked widespread public concern and drawn sharp condemnation from legal and civil society circles. Prominent human rights lawyer Femi Falana has publicly weighed in on the development, demanding the speedy prosecution of all individuals linked to the fatalities of the 37 detainees in The Punch report. Falana emphasized the necessity of accountability and transparency to ensure that justice is served for those who lost their lives while under state detention.

The establishment of the investigative panel is expected to examine the operational protocols that led to the heavy casualties within the NSCDC facility in Niger State. As authorities move forward with the administrative suspensions and inquiry, further details regarding the findings of the panel and the formal arraignment of the implicated officers remain anticipated.
