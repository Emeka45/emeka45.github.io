Lagos State Government Orders Residents in Flood-Prone Areas to Relocate Following NiHSA Alert

Following a fresh seven-day flood alert issued by the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (NiHSA), the Lagos State Government has directed residents living in low-lying and flood-prone communities to relocate immediately to safer ground.

The Lagos State Government has issued an urgent directive instructing residents living in low-lying and flood-prone communities to relocate immediately. This directive comes in the wake of a fresh seven-day flood warning released by the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (Punch Nigeria). As reported by TheCable, the precautionary measure is aimed at safeguarding lives and minimizing potential property damage across vulnerable areas in the state.

Authorities have urged citizens in these high-risk zones to take the warning seriously and move to higher ground before the full impact of the predicted flooding is felt. State agencies continue to monitor water levels and weather updates as the seven-day alert period progresses, while locals are advised to stay tuned for further updates and emergency contact instructions.
