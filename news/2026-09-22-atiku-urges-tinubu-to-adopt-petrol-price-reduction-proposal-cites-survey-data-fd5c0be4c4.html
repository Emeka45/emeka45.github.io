Atiku Urges Tinubu to Adopt Petrol Price Reduction Proposal, Cites Survey Data

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has called on President Bola Tinubu to embrace his proposal for intervention to lower petrol prices and alleviate the high cost of living, pointing to a recent SBM Intelligence survey indicating widespread public support for restoring subsidies.

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has urged President Bola Tinubu to adopt his policy proposals aimed at lowering petrol prices and easing the heavy cost of living burdens currently faced by citizens across the country (The Reporter). Atiku's call for intervention comes as economic pressures continue to dominate national discourse following significant shifts in petroleum pricing policies under the current administration.

To back his policy stance, Atiku pointed to recent findings from an SBM Intelligence survey (Punch Newspapers). According to the figures cited, 67.4 percent of Nigerians surveyed expressed support for the restoration of a petrol subsidy mechanism. The former vice president utilized these data points to justify his own alternative recommendation advocating for a production-based subsidy framework.

The ongoing national debate surrounding the removal of fuel subsidies and its direct link to inflation has remained a central political and economic issue. While the federal administration maintains that subsidy removal is a necessary step for long-term fiscal health, critics and opposition figures continue to push for targeted interventions to provide immediate relief to households.

As discussions regarding economic adjustments persist, the federal government has yet to formally alter its current petroleum pricing framework in response to the opposition leader's latest recommendations.
