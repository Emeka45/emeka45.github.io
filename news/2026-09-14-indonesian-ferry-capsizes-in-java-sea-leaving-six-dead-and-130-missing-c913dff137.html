Indonesian Ferry Capsizes in Java Sea, Leaving Six Dead and 130 Missing

A ferry traveling between Java and Borneo capsized in severe weather, prompting a large-scale search and rescue operation.

Rescue operations are underway in the Java Sea following the capsizing of the Virgo Transport 8 ferry, which encountered treacherous conditions during its voyage from Surabaya. According to officials cited by the BBC, at least six individuals lost their lives, while 130 others remain unaccounted for. Authorities confirmed that over one hundred occupants were successfully rescued from the vessel.
