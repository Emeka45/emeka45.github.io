Sega and Netflix Team Up for Crazy Taxi Film and Sonic Series

Sega and Netflix have announced a new collaborative slate that will bring classic franchises to the screen, featuring a Crazy Taxi movie alongside new Sonic the Hedgehog and Stranger Than Heaven projects.

Entertainment giant Netflix and game developer Sega have revealed a new partnership to produce multiple screen adaptations based on popular intellectual properties. As detailed by Eurogamer, the upcoming slate includes an animated television series centered on Sonic the Hedgehog, as well as feature films. According to reports from Polygon, this lineup officially includes a movie adaptation of the arcade classic Crazy Taxi, alongside a project based on Stranger Than Heaven.
