Atiku Calls on President Tinubu to Intervene and Lower Petrol and Diesel Prices

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has urged President Bola Tinubu to adopt economic interventions to lower petroleum pump prices, as citizens across Nigeria grapple with a severe cost of living crisis.

Former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has called upon President Bola Tinubu to implement targeted market interventions within the oil and gas sector to lower the pump prices of petrol and diesel. Atiku's appeal comes as Nigerians grapple with mounting economic pressures and record-high commodity costs.

According to reports detailing the Atiku proposal to reduce petrol prices, the former presidential candidate believes that government action is necessary to ease severe financial burdens on citizens. His recommendations emphasize market adjustments to help bring down petroleum product prices.

This political appeal coincides with broader economic challenges documented across the country. Reports from outlets like The Guardian Nigeria News highlight that the cost of living crisis has deepened substantially as fuel prices touch unprecedented peaks, compounding difficulties for everyday consumers.

Further reporting by The Guardian Nigeria News underscores that households across the nation are facing soaring expenses driven by continuous spikes in fuel and cooking gas rates. Concurrently, THISDAYLIVE notes that Atiku's appeal specifically targets both petrol and diesel pump pricing to alleviate widespread hardship.

As the administration weighs its options regarding macroeconomic adjustments and energy sector regulations, the official response from the presidency to Atiku's proposal remains to be seen, while citizens continue to navigate the heavy impact of inflated energy costs.
