Carney Welcomes International Leaders to Canada Amid Economic and Defense Talks

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has hosted significant diplomatic and economic events, including welcoming Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to discuss enhanced defense support and drone supplies.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has hosted significant diplomatic and economic events, including welcoming Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to discuss enhanced defense support and drone supplies, according to reports from The Guardian.

Economic Strategy and Investor Summits in Toronto

During a high-profile economic summit in Toronto involving more than 100 major investors representing over C$120tn in assets, Prime Minister Mark Carney pitched Canada as a "safe harbour" in an era marked by geopolitical and economic uncertainty. As detailed by BBC News, the prime minister has pledged to secure C$1tn ($720bn; £530bn) in investments over a five-year period to reduce Canada's traditional economic reliance on the United States.

The summit featured more than 160 projects across sectors such as critical infrastructure, digital technology, energy, and defense, generating nearly C$500bn in new investments. Ottawa introduced new tax incentives, including a "mega deduction" measure, and promised faster project approvals with a targeted one-year review timeline to offer greater predictability for major proposed developments.

Infrastructure Plans and Domestic Pushback

A major announcement coming out of the summit was Carney's plan to permit private investment in four of Canada's primary airports located in Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and Calgary. Under this initiative, the federal government would maintain ownership of the underlying land and assets while allowing private capital into airport operations to help fund broader transport infrastructure improvements.

The proposal drew sharp criticism from labor unions and advocacy groups. Representatives from the Canadian Labour Congress and the Council of Canadians argued that handing profitable public infrastructure over to private entities during an ongoing trade dispute is detrimental to workers and passengers alike.

Geopolitical Shifts and US Relations

The economic push comes amid strained trade relations with the United States, marked by sudden trade talk collapses, tit-for-tat tariffs, and a war of words. Despite the escalating economic friction and new US tariffs coming into effect, Carney addressed American executives directly in Toronto, emphasizing that Canada remains a steadfast neighbor and partner.

At the same time, Canada is actively pursuing broader international partnerships. Following the domestic summit, Carney prepared to travel to Europe to address the EU parliament and initiate discussions aimed at building a unique security and economic alliance with the bloc, signaling a strategic diversification of trade supply chains toward Europe and Asia.
