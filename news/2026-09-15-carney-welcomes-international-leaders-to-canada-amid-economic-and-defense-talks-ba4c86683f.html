Carney Welcomes International Leaders to Canada Amid Economic and Defense Talks

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has hosted significant diplomatic and economic events, including welcoming Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to discuss enhanced defense support and drone supplies.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has hosted significant diplomatic and economic events, including welcoming Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to discuss enhanced defense support and drone supplies, as reported by The Guardian.

Economic Strategy and Investment Summit

Alongside high-level diplomatic visits, Prime Minister Carney convened a major summit in Toronto to position Canada as a safe harbour for global investors amid ongoing geopolitical and economic uncertainty. Pitching to more than 100 major financiers representing substantial global assets, Carney pledged to attract C$1tn ($720bn; £530bn) in investments over a five-year period to reduce economic reliance on the United States. The summit showcased more than 160 projects across sectors such as data centres, pipelines, port expansions, and artificial intelligence infrastructure. According to the prime minister's office, the gathering generated nearly C$500bn in new investments spanning critical infrastructure, digital technology, energy, and defence.

Policy Reforms and Infrastructure Proposals

To streamline capital inflow, Ottawa introduced new tax incentives—including a proposed mega deduction—and promised faster project approvals with a target one-year review timeline to provide greater predictability. Among the most notable announcements was a plan to permit private investment in operations at four major Canadian airports in Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and Calgary, while the federal government retains ownership of the underlying land and assets. The proposal drew immediate criticism from labour unions and advocacy groups, including the Canadian Labour Congress and the Council of Canadians, who argued that handing public infrastructure over to private entities during a trade dispute runs counter to national interests.

US Trade Tensions and International Diversification

The economic push coincides with escalating trade tensions between Canada and the United States following the sudden collapse of trade talks. Tit-for-tat tariffs and new trade barriers have taken effect, prompting Canada to deepen economic and security partnerships with Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. While addressing American executives in Toronto, Carney emphasized that Canada remains a vital neighbour and partner to the United States. Concurrently, protests broke out outside the summit's opening gala, with demonstrators expressing concern over foreign ownership and resource sales.

As part of his broader international outreach, Carney prepared to travel to Europe to address the EU parliament and initiate discussions aimed at building a unique security and economic alliance, building on diplomatic engagements like his recent meetings with international leaders.
