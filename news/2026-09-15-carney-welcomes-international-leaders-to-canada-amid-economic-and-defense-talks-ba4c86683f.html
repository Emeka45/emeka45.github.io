Carney Welcomes International Leaders to Canada Amid Economic and Defense Talks

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has hosted significant diplomatic and economic events, including welcoming Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to discuss enhanced defense support and drone supplies.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has hosted high-level diplomatic and economic events, welcoming international leaders as Ottawa seeks to strengthen its global economic resilience and defense partnerships The Guardian. Amid shifting geopolitical dynamics, the administration has pursued broader international engagement, including welcoming Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to discuss enhanced defense support and drone supplies The Guardian.

Economic Resilience and Global Investment Summit

Prime Minister Carney convened a major summit in Toronto, pitching Canada as a "safe harbour" for global investors during a period of economic and geopolitical uncertainty BBC News. Addressing more than 100 major financiers managing over C$120tn in assets, the prime minister pledged to attract C$1tn ($720bn; £530bn) in investments over the next five years to reduce economic reliance on the United States BBC News.

The summit showcased more than 160 projects across sectors such as data centres, pipelines, and port expansions BBC News. Ottawa introduced new tax incentives, including a "mega deduction" measure, and promised faster project approvals featuring a one-year review timeline to establish regulatory predictability BBC News. According to the prime minister's office, the summit generated nearly C$500bn in new investments spanning critical infrastructure, digital technology, energy, and defense BBC News. AI infrastructure projects drew significant interest due to Canada's cold climate, vast space, and clean energy grid BBC News.

Infrastructure Proposals and Public Reaction

A notable announcement from the summit involved a plan to permit private investment in four major Canadian airports located in Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, and Calgary BBC News. While the federal government would maintain ownership of the underlying land and assets, the proposal aims to raise billions for transport infrastructure BBC News.

The privatization initiative drew criticism from labor unions and advocacy groups. Representatives from the Canadian Labour Congress and the Council of Canadians argued that handing public infrastructure over to private entities during a trade dispute is counterproductive BBC News. Demonstrators also gathered outside the opening night gala to protest potential sales of public resources to corporate interests BBC News.

Trade Pressures and International Pivot

The push for diversified economic ties coincides with ongoing trade disputes and tit-for-tat tariffs between Canada and the United States BBC News. Despite the strained relations, Carney emphasized that Canada remains a vital American neighbor and partner BBC News.

Looking ahead, Carney planned to travel to Europe to address the European Parliament and initiate discussions aimed at establishing a unique security and economic alliance with the European Union, marking a broader diplomatic effort to expand international trade networks BBC News.
