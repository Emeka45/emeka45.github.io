Valve Opens Reservations for Steam Frame Standalone VR Headset

Valve has officially opened reservations for its new Steam Frame virtual reality headset, revealing a starting price above $1,000 for the standalone and PC-compatible device.

Valve has officially launched the reservation waitlist for its long-awaited standalone virtual reality device, the Steam Frame. Following its initial announcement months prior, the hardware carries a base price exceeding $1,000 in the United States and roughly £889 in the UK, as detailed by Eurogamer. The newly revealed headset is designed to function both as an independent unit and as a platform for PC-to-headset streaming, though early assessments indicate mixed expectations for its standalone capabilities compared to its tethered performance.
