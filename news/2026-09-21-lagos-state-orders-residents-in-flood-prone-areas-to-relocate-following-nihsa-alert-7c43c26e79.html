Lagos State Orders Residents in Flood-Prone Areas to Relocate Following NiHSA Alert

The Lagos State Government has directed residents living in low-lying and flood-prone communities to immediately relocate, following a fresh seven-day flood warning issued by the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency.

The Lagos State Government has issued a directive instructing residents inhabiting low-lying and flood-prone areas to evacuate and relocate to safer ground. This urgent directive comes in the wake of a fresh seven-day flood alert issued by the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (NiHSA), warning of imminent flooding risks across vulnerable communities.

State authorities have emphasized the necessity of immediate compliance to prevent loss of life and minimize property damage as water levels rise. Communities identified as high-risk are being urged to take the warning seriously and move away from danger zones before conditions deteriorate further. Detailed coverage of the evacuation order can be found through The Punch.

Emergency response agencies and relevant state departments remain on high alert to monitor the situation closely and provide assistance where possible.
