Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that strongly hints at the direction and title of its upcoming sequel. The filing has surfaced shortly after the original action RPG achieved strong commercial success following its 2023 release, moving more than 4 million copies.

A Mysterious New Trademark

According to official records at the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO), publisher Neowiz has secured a trademark for a video game project titled 'Wonders of O'. Associated tags in the intellectual property filing explicitly cover interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software, and titles for network and wireless devices, confirming that the registration pertains to an upcoming interactive entertainment project.

The naming convention strongly parallels how Lies of P adapted Carlo Collodi's classic literary work The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly calling its protagonist by his traditional name. Observers point out that the 'O' in the new title likely refers to the magical land featured in L. Frank Baum's expansive book series, which begins with The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900). With fourteen distinct books making up the core Oz canon, developers have a vast literary universe to draw inspiration from.

Connecting to the Post-Credits Tease

The trademark aligns neatly with existing narrative breadcrumbs left by the development team. In the post-credits sequence of Lies of P, players discovered that the NPC previously known as Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus, a figure tied to the fate of the fictional city of Krat. Paracelsus states an intention to locate someone named Dorothy, culminating in a final shot of a figure walking across Krat's rooftops in ruby slippers and clicking their heels together.

Industry watchers suggest that the upcoming project could either bring classic Oz characters directly into the dark, gritty streets of Krat or transport the narrative toward new horizons entirely. Developer Neowiz and internal studio Nough (formerly known as Round8 Studio) have kept specific story details under wraps, though a documentary released in August hinted that the next title aims to be emotionally moving and more ambitious than its predecessor.

Development Context and Next Steps

Developers confirmed that a sequel was officially greenlit shortly after the base game's launch. Before diving entirely into the next full-scale project, the team released the Overture prequel expansion alongside updated difficulty options and brought the title to platforms like the Nintendo Switch 2.

While the sequel does not yet have a formal release window, studio director Choi Jiwon assured fans in recent documentary remarks that the team is dedicating maximum effort to the project. As development continues, fans await an official unveiling, with speculation pointing toward potential announcements at upcoming industry showcases like The Game Awards.
