Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel. The filing points strongly toward a new project carrying the title Wonders of O, continuing a naming and thematic pattern established by the original release.

Trademark Filing Hints at Oz-Inspired Direction

According to public filings at the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO), publisher Neowiz has secured protection for Wonders of O. The intellectual property notice covers interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software, and related digital assets designed for global networks and wireless devices.

While Lies of P drew heavily from Carlo Collodi's classic story The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly calling its protagonist by name, the new title strongly suggests a transition to the stories of L. Frank Baum. Baum's extensive canon of Oz books begins with The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, providing the developers with a rich source of material.

Connections to the Original Game

The thematic shift aligns closely with the post-credits sequence of the 2023 release. That cinematic revealed that the NPC known as Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus, who mentions needing to find someone named Dorothy. The final shot displayed a character walking across the rooftops of Krat wearing ruby slippers and clicking her heels together, establishing a direct bridge between the dark puppet-themed world and classic Oz iconography.

Developers Round 8 Studio (also referred to internally as Nough) confirmed shortly after the base game launched that a sequel was in development. Following the release of the base game—which has sold more than 4 million copies—the team launched the Overture prequel expansion and brought the game to the Nintendo Switch 2 alongside the DLC.

Development and Future Outlook

An August documentary surrounding the studio teased that the next installment aims to be emotionally moving and fun, reflecting an increased narrative ambition. Director Choi Jiwon noted that the internal team is dedicating massive effort to the project. Neither Neowiz nor the developers have shared a formal release window or extensive gameplay details for Wonders of O, leaving fans to await further official announcements.
