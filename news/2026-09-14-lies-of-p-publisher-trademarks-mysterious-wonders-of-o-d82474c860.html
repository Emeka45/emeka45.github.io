Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Following the successful 2023 release of the Pinocchio-inspired Soulslike title, publisher Neowiz appears to be laying the groundwork for a follow-up. According to recent filings discovered at the European Union Intellectual Property Office, the company has registered a project titled 'Wonders of O'. Industry analysts and outlets like Polygon suggest this filing points directly toward the thematic direction and literary adaptation chosen for the upcoming sequel, though official confirmation from the studio regarding gameplay details or a release window remains pending.
