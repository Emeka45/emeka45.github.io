Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel, as reported by Polygon and Eurogamer.

A New Trademark Points to Oz

The European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) recently received a trademark filing from publisher Neowiz for a video game titled Wonders of O. The intellectual property notice covers interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software, and network access tools, confirming it as a game registration.

The naming convention mirrors the approach taken with Lies of P, which adapted Carlo Collodi's The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly using the protagonist's name in the title. Similarly, Wonders of O strongly points toward L. Frank Baum's extensive Oz book series, which begins with 1900's The Wonderful Wizard of Oz.

Connecting to the Lies of P Post-Credits Tease

The trademark aligns closely with the post-credits sequence of Lies of P, which has sold more than 4 million copies since its 2023 release. That scene revealed that the NPC known as Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus, an operative who shaped the fate of Krat. Paracelsus states a need to find someone named Dorothy, followed by a shot of a figure walking on Krat rooftops in ruby slippers and clicking their heels together.

While developers Neowiz and Nough (formerly Round8 Studio) have not yet officially detailed the plot or setting of the sequel, the appearance of Dorothy in Krat leaves open whether the story will venture directly into Oz or bring classic Oz characters into the dark world of Krat.

Development Status and Studio Background

A Lies of P sequel was confirmed to be in development shortly after the base game launched. Prior to full movement on the sequel, the team released the Overture prequel expansion alongside updated difficulty options, and the game recently arrived on Nintendo Switch 2 with DLC included.

An August documentary further teased the upcoming project, with director Choi Jiwon promising fans that the next title will be emotionally moving and fun, stating that the development team is giving the project everything they have. No release window has been announced yet for Wonders of O or the Lies of P sequel.
