Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel. The original 2023 title, which offered an edgy, Pinocchio-inspired take on Carlo Collodi's classic story, has sold more than 4 million copies. Following the base game's launch, developers confirmed that a sequel was in the works, and recent trademark filings have provided the clearest indication yet of where the series is heading.

A Mysterious New Trademark

Publisher Neowiz recently filed a trademark with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) for a video game project titled 'Wonders of O', as reported by Eurogamer. The filing includes classifications for interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software, and multi-user network access, confirming that the title points directly to an upcoming game.

The naming pattern strongly suggests a connection to L. Frank Baum's famous Oz book series, which begins with The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900). Much like how Lies of P adapts The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly calling its setting or protagonist by their traditional names, a sequel could draw heavily from the 14-book Oz canon while maintaining its own distinct branding.

Tying Into the Post-Credits Tease

The trademark aligns neatly with the post-credits sequence of the first game. The scene reveals that the NPC known as Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus, who played a role in the tragedies that befell the city of Krat. After mentioning a need to find someone named Dorothy, the camera cuts to a figure walking across Krat rooftops in ruby slippers and clicking their heels together.

While developers Neowiz and Nough (formerly Round8 Studio) have not yet officially detailed the plot of the sequel, the inclusion of Dorothy and the new trademark heavily imply an Oz-themed narrative. It remains to be seen whether the sequel will transport players directly to Oz or bring Oz characters into the grim setting of Krat.

Development and Studio Goals

Ahead of the sequel's release, the team released the Overture prequel expansion—alongside much-needed difficulty and balance options—and brought the game to Nintendo Switch 2, complete with DLC included. A documentary released in August also teased that the next title will be "really fun and emotionally moving," pointing toward heightened ambition and narrative depth compared to the original game.

Internal studio Nough operates under a "games by gamers" philosophy, with director Choi Jiwon assuring fans that the team is giving everything to make the next project the best it can be.
