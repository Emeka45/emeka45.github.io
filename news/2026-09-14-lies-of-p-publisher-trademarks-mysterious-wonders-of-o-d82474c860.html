Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Publisher Neowiz has registered a brand-new trademark with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) for a mysterious video game project titled 'Wonders of O'. The filing points heavily toward the direction of the upcoming sequel to Lies of P, the hit Soulslike game that has sold more than 4 million copies since its 2023 release.

Connecting the Trademark to the Post-Credits Tease

While developer Neowiz and Nough (formerly Round8 Studio) have yet to officially share concrete details regarding the sequel's plot or release window, the naming convention strongly hints at a shift toward classic L. Frank Baum source material. Just as Lies of P drew inspiration from Carlo Collodi's The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly calling its protagonist by name, a sequel titled Wonders of O mirrors that pattern by nodding toward the numerous books set in Oz.

This follows a clear narrative breadcrumb left at the conclusion of the original game. A post-credits sequence revealed that the NPC known as Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus, a figure tied to the tragedy of Krat. Paracelsus states an intention to find someone named Dorothy, while a subsequent shot displays an individual walking across Krat rooftops in ruby slippers before clicking their heels together. Whether the sequel adapts the source material by traveling to Oz or brings Oz characters directly to Krat remains to be seen.

Development Context and Studio Ambitions

The developers confirmed that a Lies of P sequel is in the works shortly after the base game launched. Prior to the new trademark surfacing, a behind-the-scenes documentary released in August teased that the next title aims to be emotionally moving and fun, pointing to an expanded scope compared to the original title. Director Choi Jiwon noted in the documentary that the internal team is giving the project everything they have, telling players to wait just a little longer for the upcoming project.

Before moving entirely onto full-scale development for the next chapter, the team released the Overture prequel expansion alongside much-needed difficulty options and balance adjustments. Lies of P has also recently expanded to the Nintendo Switch 2, bringing the DLC package along with it.
