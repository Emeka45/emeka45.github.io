Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Publisher Neowiz, known for the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) for a mysterious project called 'Wonders of O'. The trademark filing points directly toward a video game, featuring tags for interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software, and global network access. This discovery offers the strongest glimpse yet at the direction of the upcoming sequel.

Connecting to the Wizard of Oz

The naming convention and previous hints strongly suggest that the franchise is moving from its Pinocchio roots toward the classic literary world of L. Frank Baum's Oz books. Lies of P, which launched successfully in 2023 and has sold more than 4 million copies, concluded with a post-credits scene that set up this exact transition. In the scene, the NPC Giangio reveals his true identity as Philippus Paracelsus, mentions a search for someone named Dorothy, and shows a figure walking on Krat rooftops wearing ruby slippers and clicking her heels together.

Much like how Lies of P adapted Carlo Collodi's The Adventures of Pinocchio, the upcoming project is expected to draw inspiration from the extensive Oz canon, which spans 14 books beginning with 1900's The Wonderful Wizard of Oz. Whether the sequel will transport players directly to Oz or bring Oz characters like Dorothy to the grim setting of Krat remains to be seen.

Development Progress and Studio Ambitions

Developer Neowiz and the internal team at Round8 Studio (also referred to as Nough) confirmed that a sequel was in the works shortly after the release of the first title. Recent updates, including a documentary released in August, have indicated that the next installment aims to be emotionally moving and highly entertaining, backed by an internal philosophy focused on testing and refining projects before release.

Director Choi Jiwon shared in the documentary that the team is giving everything to make the project the best it can be, promising fans that they will not be disappointed. While the sequel remains without a concrete release window, the appearance of the Wonders of O trademark on Eurogamer and additional coverage on Polygon suggest that more official details could be on the horizon.
