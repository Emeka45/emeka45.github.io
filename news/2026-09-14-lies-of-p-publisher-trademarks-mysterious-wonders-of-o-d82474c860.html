Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Publisher Neowiz has registered a brand-new trademark with the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO) for a project titled 'Wonders of O', strongly hinting at the direction of the upcoming sequel to the hit Soulslike game Lies of P.

A Mysterious Trademark and Oz Connections

The newly surfaced intellectual property filing includes tags related to interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software, and wireless device compatibility, confirming it as a video game project owned by Neowiz. The naming pattern points heavily toward L. Frank Baum's classic book series beginning with The Wonderful Wizard of Oz (1900). This connection follows a notable post-credits scene in the original 2023 title, which revealed that the NPC Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus and featured a character wearing ruby slippers on the rooftops of Krat who clicks her heels together while mentioning someone named Dorothy.

Much like how Lies of P draws inspiration from Carlo Collodi's The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly calling its protagonist Pinocchio, a potential sequel may draw from the 14 books in the Oz canon. Given that Dorothy appears heading toward Krat in the post-credits sequence, details remain open on whether the sequel will venture to Oz or bring familiar elements directly into the setting of Krat.

Development Status and Studio Ambitions

While developer Neowiz and its internal studio Nough (formerly Round8 Studio) have not yet announced an official release window or concrete plot details for the sequel, a follow-up was confirmed to be in the works shortly after the base game's launch. A documentary released in August indicated that the next project aims to be emotionally moving and fun, reflecting higher narrative ambitions from the developers. Director Choi Jiwon noted in the documentary that the team is giving the project everything it has, asking fans to wait a little longer for what promises to be a satisfying experience.

The original Lies of P achieved major commercial success, selling more than 4 million copies since its debut and establishing itself as a standout non-FromSoftware Soulslike. As the sequel enters full-scale development, fans await further official announcements from Neowiz regarding the newly trademarked title.
