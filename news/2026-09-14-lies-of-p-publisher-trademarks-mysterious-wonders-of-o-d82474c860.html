Lies of P Publisher Trademarks Mysterious 'Wonders of O'

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel.

Neowiz, the publisher behind the hit Soulslike game Lies of P, has registered a new trademark that hints at the direction of its upcoming sequel. The filing points strongly toward a new project carrying the title Wonders of O, aligning closely with previous narrative hints regarding the future of the action-heavy franchise.

Trademark Filing Points to Next Chapter

According to official records at the European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO), publisher Neowiz has secured a trademark for Wonders of O. The classification details cover interactive multimedia software, downloadable computer game software for global networks and wireless devices, and other related digital entertainment utilities, confirming the registration is for a video game project.

The naming convention mirrors the approach taken with the 2023 release Lies of P, which adapted Carlo Collodi's classic story of The Adventures of Pinocchio without explicitly naming its protagonist. Similarly, the subtitle or focus of Wonders of O strongly evokes L. Frank Baum's expansive book series beginning with The Wonderful Wizard of Oz.

Connecting to the Post-Credits Tease

The trademark heavily reinforces expectations built by the original game's conclusion. In the post-credits sequence of Lies of P, players learned that the NPC known as Giangio is actually Philippus Paracelsus, who states a need to find someone named Dorothy. A subsequent shot displays a figure walking across Krat rooftops in ruby slippers and clicking her heels together.

While developers Neowiz and Nough (formerly Round8 Studio) have not yet officially detailed the plot or confirmed whether the sequel takes place entirely within Oz or brings classic characters directly to Krat, the connection has become increasingly clear.

Development Status and Studio Ambitions

Developers confirmed that a sequel was in the works shortly after the base game launched. Lies of P has sold more than 4 million copies since its initial release and has since expanded with the Overture prequel expansion alongside much-needed difficulty options, as well as a port to the Nintendo Switch 2.

A documentary released prior to the trademark filing teased that the upcoming sequel aims to be emotionally moving and ambitious. Director Choi Jiwon noted that the internal team is giving everything to make the project live up to player expectations. No official release window has been announced for Wonders of O yet.
