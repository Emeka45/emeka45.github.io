Sony Intervenes to Halt The Last of Us Part 2 Multiplayer Mod

Development on an anticipated multiplayer modification for The Last of Us Part 2 has been abruptly stopped after PlayStation requested the creators to cease their work.

A community-driven multiplayer modification for The Last of Us Part 2, which was originally scheduled to launch this month, has been officially shut down. Development on the player-versus-player project was halted after PlayStation contacted the project leads and asked them to stop working on it.

This cancellation affects enthusiasts who were looking forward to experiencing fan-made competitive multiplayer within the acclaimed title. The intervention by Sony highlights the strict boundaries often enforced regarding unauthorized modifications of proprietary intellectual properties.
