Federal Government Suspends 20 NSCDC Officers and Niger State Commandant Over Custodial Deaths of 37 Suspected Miners

The Federal Government has suspended 20 personnel of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps, including the Niger State Commandant, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners held in custody in Minna.

The Federal Government has suspended 20 personnel of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), including the Niger State Commandant, following the tragic deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners who were held in custody in Minna The Reporter Media. The incident prompted a swift response from federal authorities, leading to wide-ranging administrative actions and the formation of an independent investigative panel.

Background and Arrests

The victims were among scores of individuals arrested during operations conducted on September 15 and 16, 2026, around the Lt Gen Mohammed Inuwa Wushishi Estate in Minna, the Niger State capital Punch Nigeria. Suberu Aniviye, who served as the NSCDC commandant in the state at the time, previously stated that the detained suspects were discovered dead in the early hours of Thursday, September 17, 2026, following a suspected outbreak of disease Punch Nigeria.

Government Action and Independent Panel

Following a directive from President Bola Tinubu for a comprehensive investigation, Minister of Interior Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo announced the suspension of the Niger State Commandant alongside 20 other personnel involved in station guard, arrest, investigation, intelligence, legal, and guard duties The Reporter Media. The Minister also constituted a 10-member independent investigative committee tasked with establishing the identities of the deceased, examining the circumstances of their arrest and detention, and determining the direct cause of death The Reporter Media.

The panel is chaired by Jonathan Kure, a retired Department of State Services Deputy Director General, with Prof. Isa Hayatu Chiroma, former Director General of the Nigerian Law School, serving as secretary The Reporter Media. Other committee members include retired AIG Hosea Hassan Karma, Prof. Olayinka Buhari, representatives from the Minna Emirate Council and the Niger State Government, Liman Sulaiman (National Secretary of the Miners Association of Nigeria), human rights activist Deji Adeyanju, journalist Zainab Suleiman Okino, and public affairs analyst George Agbakahi The Reporter Media. The committee has been given a two-week timeframe to submit its report The Reporter Media.

Compilation of Victim Records

In the wake of the tragedy, the International Human Rights Commission (IHRC) compiled and released a record of the victims' names Punch Nigeria. Awaisu Giwa, an International Peace Ambassador with the IHRC, noted that an initial list was obtained from General Hospital, Minna, through records provided by the mortuary attendant under the instruction of the Chief Medical Director, with additional names subsequently supplied by grieving relatives Punch Nigeria. The compiled records are expected to aid in ascertaining full accountability and facilitating potential compensation for the affected families Punch Nigeria.
