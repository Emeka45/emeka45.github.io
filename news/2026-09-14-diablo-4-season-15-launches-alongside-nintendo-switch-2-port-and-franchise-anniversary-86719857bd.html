Diablo 4 Launches Season of Hell's Legacy and Nintendo Switch 2 Port

Blizzard launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The major update coincides with the franchise's 30th anniversary and arrives alongside the action role-playing game's official debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. While the release faced minor hurdles, with Blizzard announcing that login issues delayed access before being resolved later that evening, players across multiple platforms were eventually able to dive into the new content.

The update rolled out globally across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X at 12:30 p.m. EDT (5:30 p.m. BST) on Tuesday, September 15, 2026. Alongside the standard platforms, the newly released Nintendo Switch 2 version arrived as the Age of Hatred Collection, which bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting cross-play and cross-progression.

New Quests, Soul Splinters, and Returning Prime Evils

Announced during BlizzCon 2026's opening ceremony, the seasonal quest line serves as a tribute to three decades of the series. The narrative features the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the original three Prime Evils have resurfaced within Sanctuary. Players must track down and battle Diablo beneath Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto deeper inside the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these formidable foes rewards players with Soul Splinters—socketable items that function similarly to gems by boosting specific stats while granting additional demon powers. However, these items also introduce a tactical trade-off by increasing the player's vulnerability to that respective demon. Additionally, the update introduces Waking Nightmares, a batch of enemy encounters designed to recreate specific moments from past entries in the franchise.

Legacy Uniques and Future Roadmap

Veterans of the franchise can also hunt down nine permanent Legacy Uniques designed to evoke nostalgia. Among these collectible items are instantly recognizable gear pieces such as Leoric's Crown, the Stone of Jordan, and The Furnace.

The update marks a busy period for the franchise following its BlizzCon showcase. Blizzard also confirmed further developments for the series moving forward, including the upcoming introduction of the Amazon class to Diablo 4 in the first half of 2027, featuring bow and javelin builds such as Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander, alongside early details regarding Diablo 5.
