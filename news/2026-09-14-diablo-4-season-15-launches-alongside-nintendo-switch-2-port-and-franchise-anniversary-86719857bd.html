Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard has launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, alongside the franchise's 30th-anniversary celebration and the game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. Revealed during BlizzCon 2026's opening ceremony, the update arrives to mark three decades of the action role-playing series, bringing notable throwback features, new mechanics, and platform expansions.

Launch Timing and Initial Hurdles

Season of Hell's Legacy officially rolled out on Tuesday, September 15, 2026. The release schedule placed the update at 12:30 p.m. EDT (9:30 a.m. PDT / 5:30 p.m. BST) across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X. However, the rollout faced initial hiccups; Blizzard announced that the launch was temporarily delayed due to login errors affecting players trying to access the Eternal and Seasonal realms. These technical issues were subsequently resolved later that same evening.

A Tribute to the Prime Evils and Deckard Cain

The anniversary update centers heavily on nostalgia, highlighted by the return of Deckard Cain's spirit. Cain warns players that echoes of the original three Prime Evils have re-emerged across Sanctuary. Veterans of earlier titles can track down these bosses in familiar locations:

  • Diablo: Housed beneath the Tristram Cathedral.
  • Baal: Located at the Arreat Summit.
  • Mephisto: Found deep within the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these formidable foes rewards players with Soul Splinter socketables. Much like gems, these items add unique effects and stat boosts to equipment—such as the Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone, which grants plus 30 to all stats and absorbs enemy souls to grant further buffs—though they simultaneously make the player more vulnerable to that specific demon. Players can also utilize modifiers and upgrades earned from these battles to feed into their War Plans.

Legacy Uniques and Waking Nightmares

To further honor the franchise's history, Season 15 introduces nine permanent Legacy Uniques that are instantly recognizable to longtime fans, including classic items like Leoric's Crown, the Stone of Jordan, and The Furnace. Additionally, the update deploys Waking Nightmares, a new batch of enemy encounters designed to recreate specific historical moments from the franchise's past.

Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Future Content

September 15 also marks the official arrival of Diablo 4 on Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection. The package bundles the base game alongside the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, offering full cross-play and cross-progression capabilities with other platform versions.

Looking ahead, Blizzard has confirmed further updates for the broader franchise pipeline, including the future addition of the Amazon class to Diablo 4 during the first half of 2027—equipped with a bow, javelin, and four distinct builds (Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, Commander)—as well as broader announcements made during BlizzCon 2026 regarding Diablo 5 and upcoming franchise projects.
