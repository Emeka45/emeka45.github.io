Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard has launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy. The major update arrived on September 15, 2026, coinciding with the franchise's 30th anniversary and the action role-playing game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2.

Anniversary Tribute and Return of Iconic Figures

Unveiled during BlizzCon's opening ceremony, the Season of Hell's Legacy serves as a tribute to three decades of the Diablo series. Developer Blizzard built the seasonal narrative around the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils—Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto—have resurfaced across Sanctuary. Players must track down these powerful enemies in familiar locations from earlier games in the franchise: Diablo is located beneath Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto deep within the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these foes grants players Soul Splinters, unique socketable items that act similarly to gems by adding passive effects and boosting specific character stats. However, utilizing these items comes with a strategic catch, as they also make characters more vulnerable to the respective demon's power. Additionally, veteran players can chase nine legacy Uniques joining the game permanently, including recognizable items such as "Leoric's Crown," the "Stone of Jordan," and "The Furnace." The update also introduces "Waking Nightmares," a batch of enemy encounters that recreate specific moments from the series' past.

Launch Timing and Technical Hurdles

The seasonal update became accessible across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X at 12:30 p.m. EDT on Tuesday, September 15, 2026 (corresponding to 9:30 a.m. PDT, 5:30 p.m. BST, and 6:30 p.m. CEST, extending into early Wednesday morning for Asian and Australian time zones). Though players can access both Eternal and Seasonal realms, Blizzard briefly announced a temporary delay on launch day due to login issues affecting players, which were successfully resolved later that same evening, as detailed by Polygon.

Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Future Content

Coinciding with the seasonal launch, Diablo 4 arrived on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection, which packages the base game alongside the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting cross-play and cross-progression. Looking ahead, Blizzard also announced that the classic Amazon class will make her first appearance in Diablo 4 during the first half of 2027, wielding a bow and javelin alongside four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander.
