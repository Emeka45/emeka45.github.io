Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The major update arrived alongside the series' 30th-anniversary celebrations and marked the franchise's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. According to details shared during the Eurogamer.net coverage of BlizzCon, the seasonal update serves as a tribute to three decades of action-RPG history by bringing back iconic characters and classic locations.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evil Rematches

The narrative of Season of Hell's Legacy centers around the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the original three Prime Evils have resurfaced across Sanctuary. Players must track down and battle Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto in familiar historical locations: the Tristram Cathedral, the Arreat Summit, and the Durance of Hate, respectively. Defeating these formidable bosses rewards players with Soul Splinters. These socketable items grant temporary boosts and borrow demonic powers, though they simultaneously increase the user's vulnerability to that specific demon.

Veteran players can also hunt down nine permanent Legacy Uniques designed to evoke nostalgia. Confirmed items for this tier include the returning Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace. Additionally, the update introduces Waking Nightmares, which are specialized enemy encounters that recreate specific sequences from past entries in the franchise.

Launch Timing and Technical Challenges

The Polygon release schedule confirmed that Season 15 officially went live on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, at 12:30 p.m. EDT (5:30 p.m. BST) across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S. However, the launch experienced immediate complications. Blizzard announced that the rollout faced temporary login errors that delayed access for players attempting to jump into the Eternal and Seasonal realms. Developer teams resolved these connectivity issues later that same evening.

Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Future Roadmap

September 15 also marked Diablo 4's hardware expansion to the Nintendo Switch 2 via The Age of Hatred Collection. This bundle packages the base game alongside both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, offering full cross-play and cross-progression capabilities with other platforms.

Looking further ahead, Blizzard used its BlizzCon announcements to outline additional franchise milestones. The classic Amazon class is officially returning to Diablo 4 in the first half of 2027, featuring bow and javelin combat styles alongside four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander.
