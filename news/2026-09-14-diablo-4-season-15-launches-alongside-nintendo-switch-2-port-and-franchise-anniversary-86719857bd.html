Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, aligning with the action role-playing game's franchise anniversary and its expansion to new hardware (Eurogamer.net). The major update made its debut alongside the Nintendo Switch 2 port, bringing a wave of nostalgic content, legacy items, and fresh gameplay mechanisms to Sanctuary.

Launch Day Disruptions and Release Schedule

The season officially targeted a launch time of 12:30 p.m. EDT on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X, translating to 5:30 p.m. BST in the U.K. (Polygon). However, player access was briefly impacted during launch day (Polygon). Blizzard announced that login errors delayed the rollout of the Season of Hell's Legacy anniversary update, though technical issues were resolved later that same evening, allowing players to access both the Eternal and Seasonal realms (Polygon).

Anniversary Tribute, Deckard Cain, and Prime Evils

Unveiled during BlizzCon's opening ceremony (Eurogamer.net), the seasonal narrative acts as a tribute to three decades of the franchise. The storyline centers around the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have resurfaced (Polygon). Players must track down and battle these iconic threats in familiar legacy locations:

  • Diablo: Located beneath Tristram Cathedral (Polygon).
  • Baal: Found at the Arreat Summit (Polygon).
  • Mephisto: Situated deep within the Durance of Hate (Polygon).

Defeating these bosses grants players modifiers and upgrades for their War Plans (Polygon).

Soul Splinters and Legacy Uniques

A core feature introduced in Season 15 is the addition of Soul Splinter socketables (Polygon). Similar to gems, these items provide equipment stat increases alongside unique effects, though they also introduce vulnerability risks tied to the respective demon (Eurogamer.net, Polygon). Additionally, the update permanently introduces nine new Legacy Uniques aimed at veteran players, featuring recognizable items such as the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace (Eurogamer.net, Polygon). Waking Nightmares encounters have also been added to recreate historic moments from the series' past (Eurogamer.net).

Nintendo Switch 2 Debut and Future Roadmaps

September 15 also marked the action role-playing game's arrival on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection, which bundles the base game with the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting cross-play and cross-progression (Eurogamer.net). Looking ahead, Blizzard confirmed that the classic Amazon class will make her first appearance in Diablo 4 during the first half of 2027, wielding a bow and javelin across four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander (Eurogamer.net).
