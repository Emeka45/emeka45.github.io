Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update arrives alongside the franchise's 30th anniversary and the action role-playing game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. Revealed during the BlizzCon 2026 opening ceremony, the milestone update brings back iconic elements from the series' history, including the return of Deckard Cain and a rematch against the original three Prime Evils.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evil Rematches

During the BlizzCon opening ceremony, developer Blizzard detailed how the new season serves as a tribute to three decades of Diablo. The storyline features the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the original Prime Evils have resurfaced across Sanctuary. Players must track down and battle Diablo beneath Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto within the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these powerful foes rewards players with Soul Splinter socketables, items that grant a portion of a demon's power while simultaneously increasing vulnerability to that same demon. According to reports detailing the Diablo 4 Season 15 release time, specific items like the "Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone" can provide stat bonuses alongside unique mechanics, such as absorbing enemy souls to further buff the character.

Legacy Uniques and Nintendo Switch 2 Launch

To celebrate the anniversary, the update permanently introduces nine new Legacy Uniques designed to be instantly recognizable to veteran players. Confirmed items include returning classics like the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace, alongside Waking Nightmares encounters that recreate specific moments from the series' past.

Coinciding with the seasonal launch, Diablo 4 officially landed on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection. The package includes the base game alongside both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, supporting cross-play and cross-progression with other platforms.

Launch Timing and Initial Technical Hurdles

The update became available globally on September 15, 2026, targeting platforms including Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X at 12:30 p.m. EDT (5:30 p.m. BST). However, the rollout experienced minor disruptions; Blizzard announced that the update's launch was temporarily delayed due to login issues affecting players on both Eternal and Seasonal realms. These technical difficulties were successfully resolved later that same evening.
