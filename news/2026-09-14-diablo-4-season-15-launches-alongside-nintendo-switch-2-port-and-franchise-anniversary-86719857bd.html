Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard launched Diablo 4's Season 15, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The major update coincides with the franchise's 30th anniversary and arrives alongside the action role-playing game's official debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. While the rollout experienced minor hiccups due to login errors that temporarily delayed access to the Eternal and Seasonal realms, the issues were resolved by Blizzard later that evening.

Anniversary Content and Return of the Prime Evils

Unveiled during BlizzCon's opening ceremony, Season of Hell's Legacy serves as a tribute to three decades of the franchise. The narrative centers on the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have reemerged in Sanctuary. To push back these threats, players must venture into iconic locations from earlier games: Tristram Cathedral to face Diablo, Arreat Summit to battle Baal, and the Durance of Hate to locate Mephisto.

Defeating these formidable bosses rewards players with Soul Splinter socketables. These items grant equipment stat bonuses and special effects—such as collecting enemy souls to buff characters—though they also introduce a trade-off by making the user more vulnerable to that specific demon. Additionally, the season introduces Waking Nightmares, a series of enemy encounters recreating historical moments from Diablo's past, alongside nine permanent legacy Uniques instantly recognizable to veterans, including Leoric's Crown, The Furnace, and a returning Stone of Jordan.

Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Future Roadmap

The Nintendo Switch 2 version launched simultaneously on September 15, arriving as the Age of Hatred Collection. This physical code-in-the-box release bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, offering full cross-play and cross-progression support with other platforms.

BlizzCon 2026 also provided a glimpse further into the future for fans of the action-RPG series. Blizzard confirmed plans to introduce the classic Amazon class to Diablo 4 in the first half of 2027, complete with a bow, javelin, and four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander. Furthermore, developer announcements outlined broader franchise milestones, including updates on Diablo 5 and a confirmation that no further expansions are planned for Diablo 4 prior to its eventual successor's release.
