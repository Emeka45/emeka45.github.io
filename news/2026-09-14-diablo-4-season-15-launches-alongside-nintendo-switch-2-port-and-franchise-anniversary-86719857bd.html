Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment launched the 15th season of Diablo 4, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, alongside the game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. The major update arrived to coincide with the franchise's 30th anniversary, bringing nostalgic elements, new items, and platform expansions to the action role-playing game.

Anniversary Content and Return of the Prime Evils

During BlizzCon's opening ceremony, Blizzard detailed that the anniversary season serves as a tribute to three decades of Diablo. The seasonal quest line features the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have resurfaced within Sanctuary. Players must track down and battle Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto in iconic locations familiar to veterans of earlier games in the series: Tristram Cathedral, the Arreat Summit, and the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these powerful enemies yields Soul Splinter socketables, which allow players to borrow a portion of a demon's power while simultaneously increasing their vulnerability to that same entity. Additionally, the update introduces Waking Nightmares, a batch of enemy encounters designed to recreate specific moments from the franchise's past.

New Uniques and Nintendo Switch 2 Port

Veterans can also chase nine permanent Legacy Uniques coming with Diablo 4 Season 15, featuring instantly recognizable items such as Leoric's Crown, the Stone of Jordan, and The Furnace.

Coinciding with the seasonal rollout, the action role-playing game made its official Nintendo Switch 2 debut through the Age of Hatred Collection. This release packages the base game alongside both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, supporting full cross-play and cross-progression with other available versions.

Launch Timing and Initial Disruptions

The update was slated to go live globally on September 15, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. BST (12:30 p.m. EDT / 9:30 a.m. PDT). However, the launch experienced brief setbacks when login errors delayed access to the Eternal and Seasonal realms. Blizzard subsequently confirmed and resolved the issues later that same evening, allowing players to access both realms smoothly.

Looking further ahead, Blizzard also announced that the classic Amazon character class will make her first appearance in Diablo 4 during the first half of 2027, wielding a bow and javelin across four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander.
