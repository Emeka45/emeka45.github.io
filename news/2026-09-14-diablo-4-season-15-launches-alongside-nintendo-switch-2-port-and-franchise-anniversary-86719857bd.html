Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment's Diablo 4 has launched its milestone 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy. The major update rolled out on September 15, 2026, coinciding with the 30th anniversary of the Diablo franchise and bringing the action role-playing game to Nintendo hardware for the first time.

Launch Details and Regional Timing

The update became available across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X on September 15, 2026, though the rollout experienced minor hiccups. According to Polygon, login errors briefly delayed player access on the Eternal and Seasonal realms before Blizzard resolved the server issues later that same evening. Globally, the launch times varied by region:

  • 12:30 p.m. EDT (East Coast North America)
  • 1:30 p.m. BRT (Brazil)
  • 5:30 p.m. BST (U.K.)
  • 6:30 p.m. CEST (Western Europe)
  • 1:30 a.m. JST on Sept. 16 (Japan)
  • 2:30 a.m. AEST on Sept. 16 (East Coast Australia)

New Content, Bosses, and Soul Splinters

Season of Hell's Legacy introduces a fresh seasonal quest line that tasks players with closing Ruptures across Sanctuary rather than battling the Death Cult. The narrative features the returning spirit of Deckard Cain, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have returned. Players can track down and battle these iconic antagonists in familiar locations: Diablo beneath Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto deep within the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these powerful bosses yields Soul Splinters, unique socketable items that provide stat bonuses and unique combat effects—such as absorbing enemy souls—while simultaneously increasing vulnerability against the corresponding demon. Additionally, the update adds nine permanent legacy Uniques designed to appeal to series veterans, featuring instantly recognizable items like the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace, alongside Waking Nightmares encounters that recreate past moments from the franchise.

Nintendo Switch 2 Debut and Future Roadmaps

September 15 also marked the long-awaited Nintendo Switch 2 release via the Age of Hatred Collection. The package includes the base game alongside both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, supporting cross-play and cross-progression with other platforms.

During BlizzCon 2026's opening ceremony, Blizzard also provided a look at future updates. The classic class the Amazon is scheduled to return to the franchise in the first half of 2027, wielding bows and javelins across four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander. These announcements formed part of a broader slate of franchise reveals that included news regarding Diablo 5.
