Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard has launched Diablo 4 Season 15, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, coinciding with the franchise's 30th anniversary and the game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. Revealed during the BlizzCon opening ceremony, the major update brings back iconic elements from the series' history, including the return of Deckard Cain and a rematch against the original Prime Evils.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evil Rematches

During BlizzCon, developer Blizzard detailed that the new season serves as a tribute to three decades of Diablo. The storyline features the spirit of Deckard Cain returning to warn players that echoes of the three Prime Evils have resurfaced across Sanctuary. Players must track down and defeat Diablo at Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto in the Durance of Hate.

Overcoming these foes rewards players with Soul Splinters—socketable items that grant demon powers while simultaneously increasing vulnerability to that specific demon. Additionally, the update introduces nine permanent Legacy Uniques recognizable to series veterans, such as the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace, alongside Waking Nightmares that recreate historical moments from past titles.

Nintendo Switch 2 Debut and Release Details

Marking its platform debut, Diablo 4 arrived on the Nintendo Switch 2 as the Age of Hatred Collection, which bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting cross-play and cross-progression.

According to Polygon, Season 15 officially went live on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X. Launch times included 12:30 p.m. EDT / 9:30 a.m. PDT in North America, 5:30 p.m. BST in the U.K., and 6:30 p.m. CEST in western Europe, followed by early morning hours on September 16 in Japan and Australia. Although initial login errors and server issues delayed access for some players on launch day, Blizzard resolved the technical hurdles later that same evening.

Future Roadmap

Looking ahead into the first half of 2027, the classic Amazon character class is slated to make her Diablo 4 debut, wielding bows and javelins with four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander.
