Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. Revealed during the BlizzCon opening ceremony, the update arrives alongside the franchise's 30th-anniversary celebrations and the game's official debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. While the release encountered temporary login issues that delayed player access on launch day, Blizzard successfully resolved the server hurdles later that evening, allowing players to jump into the Eternal and Seasonal realms.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evil Rematches

The theme of Season 15 serves as a direct tribute to three decades of the franchise. According to details covered by Eurogamer.net and Polygon, the seasonal questline brings back the spirit of Deckard Cain to warn players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have resurfaced across Sanctuary. Players must track down and defeat Diablo beneath Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto deep within the Durance of Hate.

Succeeding in these battles grants players Soul Splinter socketables, which function similarly to gems by adding distinct effects and stat increases to equipment. For instance, the Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone from the 3.2.0 PTR provided plus 30 to all stats while collecting enemy souls to further buff the character. However, these soul splinters introduce a trade-off by making characters more vulnerable to the respective demon's power.

Legacy Uniques and Switch 2 Debut

Veteran players can also hunt down nine permanent legacy Uniques designed to evoke nostalgia from earlier series entries. Confirmed items include returning fan-favorites such as the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace, alongside Waking Nightmares that recreate specific historical moments from the franchise's past.

Simultaneously, Diablo 4 reaches Nintendo's hardware via the Nintendo Switch 2. The platform debut arrives via the Age of Hatred Collection, which bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while fully supporting cross-play and cross-progression with other platforms. Blizzard also announced that the classic Amazon class will make her return to the franchise in the first half of 2027, wielding a bow and javelin across four specialized builds.
