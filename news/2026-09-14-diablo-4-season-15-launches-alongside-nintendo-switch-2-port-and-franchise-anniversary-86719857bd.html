Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The major update coincides with the 30th anniversary of the Diablo franchise and marks the action role-playing game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2, as detailed during the BlizzCon 2026 opening ceremony.

Launch Timing and Initial Hurdles

The update was scheduled to release globally across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X at 12:30 p.m. EDT (5:30 p.m. BST) on Tuesday, September 15. However, the rollout faced temporary login errors that delayed player access. Blizzard later confirmed that the issues affecting the Eternal and Seasonal realms were resolved later that same evening.

New Content and Anniversary Themes

Season of Hell's Legacy acts as a tribute to three decades of the franchise, bringing back iconic figures like Deckard Cain. Cain's spirit returns to warn players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils—Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto—have resurfaced in Sanctuary. Players must hunt these foes down in familiar legacy locations: Tristram Cathedral for Diablo, the Arreat Summit for Baal, and the Durance of Hate for Mephisto.

Defeating these bosses yields Soul Splinters, powerful socketable items similar to gems that grant specific demonic powers while simultaneously increasing the player's vulnerability to that same demon. According to reporting from Polygon, items like the "Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone" from the Public Test Realm offered substantial stat increases alongside special soul-absorbing mechanics. Additionally, the update introduces nine permanent Legacy Uniques recognizable to franchise veterans, including returning fan-favorite items such as the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace, alongside Waking Nightmares encounters that recreate moments from the series' past.

Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Future Roadmap

Coinciding with the seasonal launch, Eurogamer reports that Diablo 4 arrives on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection. This version bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting cross-play and cross-progression with other platforms.

Looking ahead, Blizzard also announced that the classic Amazon class will make her first appearance in Diablo 4 during the first half of 2027, featuring a bow and javelin alongside four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander.
