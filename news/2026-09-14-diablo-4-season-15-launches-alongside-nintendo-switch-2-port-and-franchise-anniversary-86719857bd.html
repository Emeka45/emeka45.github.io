Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The major update arrives alongside the franchise's 30th anniversary celebrations and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2. According to details shared during the Eurogamer coverage of BlizzCon, the new seasonal content serves as a tribute to three decades of the series, built around the return of Deckard Cain and a rematch with the original Prime Evils.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evil Rematches

During the opening ceremony at BlizzCon, developers outlined how Cain's spirit returns to warn players that echoes of the three Prime Evils have resurfaced within Sanctuary. Players must track down these threats in familiar locations tied to the franchise's history: Tristram Cathedral to face Diablo, Arreat Summit to fight Baal, and the Durance of Hate to locate Mephisto.

Defeating these powerful adversaries yields a Soul Splinter, allowing players to borrow a portion of a demon's power, though doing so also increases vulnerability to that same demon. Reporting from Polygon notes that socketables like the "Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone" can grant substantial stat boosts and absorb enemy souls to further buff the character.

Legacy Uniques and Nintendo Switch 2 Launch

Veteran players can also chase nine permanent new Legacy Uniques that evoke nostalgic memories from past titles, including returning favorites like Leoric's Crown, The Furnace, and the Stone of Jordan. Additionally, a batch of enemy encounters known as Waking Nightmares recreates specific moments from Diablo's past.

The update coincides with the game's official debut on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection, which bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting cross-play and cross-progression.

Launch Timing and Initial Hurdles

Season of Hell's Legacy officially released on September 15, 2026, across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X at 12:30 p.m. EDT (5:30 p.m. BST). The rollout experienced minor disruptions when login issues temporarily delayed access to the Eternal and Seasonal realms, though Blizzard resolved the technical hurdles later that same evening.
