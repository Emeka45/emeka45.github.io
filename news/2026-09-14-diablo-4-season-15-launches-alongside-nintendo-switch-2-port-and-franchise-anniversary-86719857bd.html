Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard Entertainment launched Diablo 4 Season 15, officially titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. According to reports from Eurogamer and Polygon, the major content update coincides with the franchise's 30th anniversary and arrives alongside the action role-playing game's official debut on the Nintendo Switch 2.

Launch Timing and Initial Issues

Season of Hell's Legacy was scheduled to go live at 5:30 p.m. BST (12:30 p.m. EDT) on Tuesday, September 15, across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S. However, the rollout faced initial hiccups; Blizzard announced that the update's launch experienced login errors that delayed player access to the Eternal and Seasonal realms, though the technical difficulties were successfully resolved later that same evening.

Anniversary Themes, Quests, and Soul Splinters

Unveiled during BlizzCon's opening ceremony, the anniversary season serves as a tribute to three decades of the franchise. The narrative brings back the spirit of Deckard Cain to warn players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils—Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto—have returned to Sanctuary.

Players must track down these iconic adversaries in familiar historical locations:

  • Diablo: Located beneath the Tristram Cathedral
  • Baal: Located at the Arreat Summit
  • Mephisto: Located deep within the Durance of Hate

Defeating these foes grants players Soul Splinters, unique socketable items that provide stat increases and special powers—such as the Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone, which absorbs defeated enemies to buff the player. The mechanic introduces a risk-and-reward element, as utilizing a demon's power also increases vulnerability to that same entity. Additionally, players can encounter Waking Nightmares, a batch of enemy encounters that recreate specific scenarios from past games.

Legacy Uniques and Nintendo Switch 2 Port

Veterans of the franchise can also hunt down nine permanent new Legacy Uniques designed to be instantly recognizable, including the returning Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace.

September 15 also marks the long-awaited Nintendo Switch 2 release of Diablo 4 via the Age of Hatred Collection. The bundle includes the base game alongside both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, complete with cross-play and cross-progression support across platforms.
