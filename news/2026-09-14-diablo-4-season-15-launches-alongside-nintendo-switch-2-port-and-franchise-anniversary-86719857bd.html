Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The major update arrived alongside the franchise's 30th-anniversary celebrations and marked the action role-playing game's official debut on the Nintendo Switch 2, as detailed during BlizzCon 2026's opening ceremony.

Launch and Technical Hurdles

The update was slated to go live at 5:30 p.m. UK time (12:30 p.m. EDT) on Tuesday, September 15, across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S. Global rollout times varied by region, including 9:30 a.m. PDT for the west coast of North America, 6:30 p.m. CEST for western Europe, and early morning hours on September 16 for Japan and Australia. However, login errors briefly delayed access to the Eternal and Seasonal realms before Blizzard resolved the issues later that evening.

The Return of Deckard Cain and the Prime Evils

During BlizzCon 2026, developer Blizzard announced that the new season serves as a tribute to three decades of the franchise. The seasonal quest line brings back the spirit of Deckard Cain, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have resurfaced in Sanctuary.

Players must track down these powerful enemies in familiar locations from earlier games in the series:

  • Diablo: Located beneath Tristram Cathedral
  • Baal: Found at the Arreat Summit
  • Mephisto: Housed deep within the Durance of Hate

Defeating these foes rewards players with Soul Splenter socketables—such as the Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone from the PTR—which grant stat boosts and special effects like absorbing enemy souls, though they simultaneously make the character more vulnerable to that specific demon.

Legacy Uniques and Nintendo Switch 2 Port

Veterans of the franchise can also hunt down nine permanent Legacy Uniques designed to evoke nostalgia, including instantly recognizable items like Leoric's Crown, the Stone of Jordan, and The Furnace. Additionally, enemy encounters known as Waking Nightmares recreate specific historic moments from Diablo's past.

Coinciding with the seasonal launch, Diablo 4 arrived on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection, which includes the base game alongside the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions with full cross-play and cross-progression support. Blizzard also announced that the classic Amazon class will return to the game in the first half of 2027.
