Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard has launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, marking both the franchise's 30th anniversary and the action role-playing game's arrival on the Nintendo Switch 2. Revealed during the BlizzCon opening ceremony Eurogamer.net, the major update brings back iconic series elements, including the return of Deckard Cain's spirit and a rematch against the original three Prime Evils.

Release Timing and Launch Challenges

Season of Hell's Legacy officially went live on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, across Battle.net, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, and the new Nintendo Switch 2 hardware Polygon. Rollout times included 12:30 p.m. EDT / 9:30 a.m. PDT in North America, 5:30 p.m. BST in the U.K., and 6:30 p.m. CEST in western Europe, with regional times extending into September 16 for Japan and Australia. However, the launch experienced initial friction; login errors delayed player access to the new Eternal and Seasonal realms before Blizzard resolved the issues later that same evening Polygon.

Prime Evils, Soul Splinters, and Legacy Uniques

The seasonal questline moves players away from fighting the Death Cult and into closing various Ruptures across Sanctuary Polygon. Cain's spirit returns to warn players that echoes of Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto have surfaced in familiar locations from earlier games: Tristram Cathedral, the Arreat Summit, and the Durance of Hate Eurogamer.net.

Defeating these bosses rewards players with Soul Splinters, socketable items that borrow demonic power to boost stats and add effects—such as the Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone, which can grant plus 30 to all stats and absorb enemy souls—though they also make characters more vulnerable to that demon Eurogamer.net, Polygon. Additionally, the update introduces nine permanent Legacy Uniques instantly recognizable to veterans, including the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace, alongside Waking Nightmares that recreate historical moments from the franchise's past Eurogamer.net, Polygon.

Nintendo Switch 2 Debut and Future Roadmap

Alongside the seasonal update, Diablo 4 debuted on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection, which bundles the base game with the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions while supporting full cross-play and cross-progression Eurogamer.net. Blizzard also announced that the classic Amazon character class will make her first appearance in Diablo 4 during the first half of 2027, wielding bows and javelins across four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander Eurogamer.net.
