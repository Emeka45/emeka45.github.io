Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Blizzard has launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, alongside the game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2 and celebrations for the franchise's 30th anniversary. Detailed during BlizzCon's opening ceremony, the update went live on September 15, 2026, though its initial rollout experienced temporary login errors that delayed player access before being resolved later that evening.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evils

Acting as a tribute to three decades of the franchise, the new seasonal quest line brings back the spirit of Deckard Cain to warn players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have resurfaced in Sanctuary. Players must hunt down and battle these iconic bosses in familiar locations from earlier games: Diablo in the Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto deep within the Durance of Hate. Defeating them rewards players with Soul Splinters, which grant powerful demon abilities while simultaneously increasing vulnerability to that same demon. Additionally, the update introduces nine permanent Legacy Uniques recognizable to series veterans—such as Leoric's Crown, the Stone of Jordan, and The Furnace—alongside Waking Nightmares encounters that recreate past moments from the series.

Nintendo Switch 2 Debut and Expansion Launch

Coinciding with the seasonal update, Diablo 4 officially arrives on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection. This release packages the base game together with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, offering full cross-play and cross-progression support with other platform versions.

Release Schedule
