Diablo 4 Season 15 Launches Alongside Nintendo Switch 2 Port and Franchise Anniversary

Blizzard is set to launch Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, on September 15, 2026. The update coincides with the series' 30th anniversary and brings the action role-playing game to the Nintendo Switch 2.

Diablo 4 Launches Season of Hell's Legacy and Nintendo Switch 2 Port

Blizzard has launched Diablo 4's 15th season, titled Season of Hell's Legacy, coinciding with the franchise's 30th-anniversary celebrations and the game's debut on the Nintendo Switch 2. The update rolled out following announcements made during BlizzCon's opening ceremony, bringing a wave of nostalgic content, new mechanics, and platform expansions to the action role-playing game as detailed by Eurogamer.

Originally slated to drop at 12:30 p.m. EDT (5:30 p.m. BST) on September 15, 2026, across platforms including Battle.net, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X, the seasonal update encountered minor hurdles. According to Polygon, login issues delayed player access to the Eternal and Seasonal realms, though Blizzard resolved the server complications later that same evening.

Anniversary Content and Prime Evil Rematches

Season of Hell's Legacy serves as a tribute to three decades of the franchise. The seasonal quest line features the return of Deckard Cain's spirit, who warns players that echoes of the three original Prime Evils have resurfaced across Sanctuary. Players must track down and battle Diablo beneath Tristram Cathedral, Baal at the Arreat Summit, and Mephisto deep within the Durance of Hate.

Defeating these powerful foes rewards players with Soul Splinters—socketable items functioning similarly to gems that grant specific stat increases and demon powers, such as the Greater Splinter of the Black Soultone. However, these items also introduce a trade-off by making characters more vulnerable to that specific demon. Additionally, the update introduces Waking Nightmares, a series of enemy encounters recreating moments from the series' past, alongside nine permanent Legacy Uniques recognizable to veterans, including the Stone of Jordan, Leoric's Crown, and The Furnace.

Nintendo Switch 2 Debut and Future Roadmaps

Alongside the seasonal content, Diablo 4 arrives on the Nintendo Switch 2 via the Age of Hatred Collection. The package bundles the base game with both the Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred expansions, offering full cross-play and cross-progression support with other versions.

The BlizzCon 2026 announcements also outlined long-term plans for the franchise, including the future introduction of the Amazon class to Diablo 4 in the first half of 2027, featuring bow and javelin combat alongside four distinct builds: Champion, Skirmisher, Ranger, and Commander. Furthermore, Blizzard showcased major franchise milestones during the event, offering early reveals for Diablo 5, World of Warcraft: Forever, and a new open-world StarCraft shooter.
