Federal Government and NSCDC Take Action: Officers Suspended and Arrested Over Niger Miners' Deaths

The Federal Government and the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps have taken stringent measures, suspending 20 officers including the Niger State Commandant and arresting a total of 24 personnel following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners in custody.

The Federal Government has taken decisive action in response to a major security scandal, suspending 20 officers of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps alongside the Niger State Commandant. The high-level disciplinary measures follow the tragic deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners while in custody, an incident that has drawn intense scrutiny toward custodial safety and operational protocols within the agency.

As part of the ongoing administrative response, a specialized investigative panel has been established to uncover the full circumstances surrounding the fatalities. Complementing the federal suspensions, internal enforcement actions have continued to gather pace.
