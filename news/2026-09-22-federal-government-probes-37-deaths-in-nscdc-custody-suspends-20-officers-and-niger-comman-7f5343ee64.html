Federal Government Probes 37 Deaths in NSCDC Custody, Suspends 20 Officers and Niger Commandant

The Federal Government has launched an independent panel and suspended 20 Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps officers, including the Niger State Commandant, following the deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners held in custody.

The Federal Government has taken decisive administrative and investigative actions following the tragic deaths of 37 suspected illegal miners while in the custody of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) in Niger State. As part of these measures, authorities have established an independent investigative committee and suspended multiple personnel linked to the facility (Punch Newspapers).

In response to the incident, the government announced the immediate suspension of 20 NSCDC officers, which notably includes the Niger State Commandant (The Reporter). Officials have vowed that there will be no cover-up in the ongoing efforts to uncover the circumstances surrounding the fatalities of the detainees, who were reportedly held on allegations of illegal mining activities.

The newly inaugurated independent panel is tasked with thoroughly examining the events that transpired in the custody facility and determining accountability. Further developments and findings from the investigative committee are expected as the probe continues.
