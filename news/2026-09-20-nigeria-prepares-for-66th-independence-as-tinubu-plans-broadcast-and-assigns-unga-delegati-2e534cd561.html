Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

As Nigeria prepares to mark its 66th Independence Anniversary, President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address the nation in a nationwide broadcast on October 1, 2026. The Federal Government has outlined a low-key programme for the milestone celebration, coordinating activities through a newly inaugurated inter-ministerial committee.

Anniversary Planning and Schedule of Events

The inter-ministerial committee responsible for coordinating the anniversary events is chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. During the inaugural meeting, Akume was represented by Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office.

According to the The Reporter coverage, Akume emphasized that the anniversary offers a moment for citizens to reflect on the contributions, sacrifices, and legacies of the nation's founding figures who fought for independence from colonial rule. He added that the commemoration will serve as a platform for the administration to showcase its achievements and highlight reform efforts aimed at addressing current socio-economic challenges.

Due to limited time before the anniversary, the committee was instructed to keep the events low-key and execute them with urgency. The lined-up programme features:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: President Tinubu's nationwide broadcast

The planning committee brings together top government functionaries, including the Ministers of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Information and National Orientation, Interior, Foreign Affairs, Federal Capital Territory, and Budget and Economic Planning. Other members include the National Security Adviser, Inspector General of Police, Director General of the Department of State Services, Commander of the Guards Brigade, and various permanent secretaries.

Vice President Shettima Assigned to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a related development, President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, United States, ending weeks of speculation regarding the president's attendance as reported by The Punch.

The Presidency's statement, issued by Stanley Nkwocha, noted that Vice President Shettima's delegation includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials. The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme "Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all."

During the UNGA week, Vice President Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement detailing the country's positions on global issues affecting national development, peace, security, economic growth, and international cooperation. He will also participate in high-level discussions focusing on climate action, the Right to Development, and strategic bilateral meetings with global leaders and international development partners to advance economic diplomacy and sustainable development.
