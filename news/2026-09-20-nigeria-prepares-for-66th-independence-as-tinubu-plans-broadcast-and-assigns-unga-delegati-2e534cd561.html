Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address citizens on October 1 as part of a low-key programme arranged for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, according to The Reporter. Alongside preparations for the national landmark, the administration has structured coordination details and mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the country's delegation to international engagements abroad.

Preparations for the 66th Independence Anniversary

The Federal Government inaugurated an Inter-Ministerial Committee to coordinate activities for the 66th anniversary, chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. Akume was represented at the inaugural meeting by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens.

Organisers noted that the anniversary will allow citizens to reflect on the contributions, sacrifices, and legacies of founding figures who fought for independence from colonial rule. It also serves as a platform for the administration to showcase its achievements and reform efforts amidst socio-economic challenges. However, the SGF directed that the events remain low-key due to limited time before October 1.

The commemorative schedule includes:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: Nationwide broadcast by President Tinubu

The planning committee features senior officials including ministers overseeing finance, information, interior, foreign affairs, the Federal Capital Territory, and budget planning, alongside the National Security Adviser, Inspector General of Police, Director General of the Department of State Services, and the Commander of the Guards Brigade.

Vice President Shettima Mandated to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a related development reported by Punch, President Tinubu mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States. This decision resolves prior discussions following Tinubu's departure to Europe for a three-week leave starting August 30.

The high-level Nigerian delegation led by Shettima includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials. The UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28 under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.”

During the summit, Vice President Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement, address issues concerning the Right to Development and climate action, attend the traditional reception for heads of delegation, and participate in bilateral meetings with global leaders and international partners to advance economic diplomacy and multilateral cooperation.
