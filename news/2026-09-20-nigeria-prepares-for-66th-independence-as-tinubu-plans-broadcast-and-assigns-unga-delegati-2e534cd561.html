Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians in a nationwide broadcast on October 1 as part of a low-key programme arranged for the country’s 66th Independence Anniversary. In tandem with preparations for the national milestone, the presidency has also announced that Vice President Kashim Shettima will lead Nigeria’s high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States.

Anniversary Planning and Schedule

To coordinate events for the 66th Independence Anniversary, the Federal Government inaugurated an Inter-Ministerial Committee chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. At the inaugural meeting, Akume was represented by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens.

According to The Reporter, the SGF noted that the anniversary provides an opportunity to honor the contributions and sacrifices of Nigeria's founding fathers and heroines who fought for independence from colonial rule. He also stated that the celebration would highlight the administration's reform efforts and achievements amidst current socio-economic challenges.

Due to limited time before October 1, the SGF directed the committee to keep the schedule low-key. The lined-up events feature:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: Nationwide broadcast by President Bola Tinubu

The committee brings together key government figures, including ministers across finance, information, interior, foreign affairs, the Federal Capital Territory, and budget planning, alongside security chiefs such as the Inspector General of Police, the Director General of the Department of State Services, and the Commander of the Guards Brigade.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

As outlined in a statement issued by Stanley Nkwocha, Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Communications in the Office of the Vice President, President Tinubu mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him at the 81st UNGA session, as reported by Punch. The directive closes weeks of discussions regarding whether the president would attend the gathering personally following his departure from Abuja on August 30 for a three-week European leave.

Shettima's high-level delegation includes Rivers State Governor Siminalayi Fubara, Zamfara State Governor Dauda Lawal, ministers, and other senior government officials. Running under the General Debate theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all,” the UNGA schedule spans from September 22 to 28, 2026.

During the summit, Vice President Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement, address critical issues regarding peace, security, economic growth, and climate action, and engage in bilateral discussions with international partners to advance economic diplomacy and sustainable development.
