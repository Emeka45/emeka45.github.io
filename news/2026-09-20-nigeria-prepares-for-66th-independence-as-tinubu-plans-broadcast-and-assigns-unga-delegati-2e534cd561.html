Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

As Nigeria prepares to mark its 66th Independence Anniversary, President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address the nation in a nationwide broadcast on October 1, 2026. The Federal Government has structured the commemoration as a low-key programme, reflecting the limited time available for preparations following the inauguration of a dedicated Inter-Ministerial Committee.

Independence Anniversary Preparations

The planning committee for the 66th anniversary is chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. At the inaugural meeting, Akume was represented by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens. According to the The Reporter, the SGF emphasized that the anniversary offers a moment to reflect on the sacrifices made by Nigeria's founding figures, while also highlighting the administration's ongoing reforms and achievements in addressing national socio-economic challenges.

The lined-up schedule of events comprises:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: President Tinubu's nationwide broadcast

The inter-ministerial body includes various senior officials, security chiefs, and ministers overseeing finance, information, interior, foreign affairs, the Federal Capital Territory, and budget planning, alongside the National Security Adviser, the Inspector General of Police, the Director General of the Department of State Services, and the Commander of the Guards Brigade.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In tandem with domestic anniversary preparations, the Presidency announced that President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead Nigeria’s high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States. Reporting via The Punch, the deployment follows weeks of public speculation regarding the president's attendance, which arose after Tinubu departed Abuja on August 30 for a three-week European leave spanning London and France.

The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the central theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.” Vice President Shettima's delegation includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials.

During the summit, Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement addressing domestic priorities on peace, security, economic growth, and international cooperation. His schedule also features participation in high-level discussions concerning climate action, the Right to Development, traditional head-of-delegation receptions, and bilateral meetings aimed at advancing economic diplomacy and sustainable development finance.
