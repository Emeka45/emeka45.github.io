Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is set to address Nigerians on October 1, 2026, as part of a low-key programme arranged for the nation's 66th Independence Anniversary. Alongside preparations for the domestic milestone, the presidency has assigned Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, United States.

66th Independence Anniversary Preparations

To coordinate events for the anniversary, the Federal Government inaugurated an inter-ministerial committee chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. Represented at the inaugural meeting by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, Akume noted that the anniversary offers citizens a chance to reflect on the legacies and sacrifices of Nigeria's founding figures. He added that the programme will showcase the administration's reform efforts and achievements amidst current socio-economic challenges.

Due to time constraints, the SGF directed the committee to keep celebrations low-key. The lined-up events begin with a World Press Conference on September 24, followed by a Juma’at Service on September 25 and a Church Service on September 27. An Independence Day Public Lecture is scheduled for September 30, culminating in President Tinubu’s nationwide broadcast on October 1, as reported by The Reporter.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a related development, President Tinubu mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and head the Nigerian delegation to the 81st UNGA session. The directive resolves weeks of speculation regarding the president's attendance following his departure for a three-week European vacation on August 30.

According to a statement issued by Stanley Nkwocha, Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Communications in the Office of the Vice President, Shettima's high-level delegation includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, alongside ministers and senior government officials. Additional details on the delegation and schedule were covered by Punch.

The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, centered on the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.” Vice President Shettima is scheduled to deliver Nigeria's national statement, engage in high-level meetings concerning climate action and the Right to Development, and participate in bilateral talks with world leaders and international partners to advance economic diplomacy and multilateral cooperation.
