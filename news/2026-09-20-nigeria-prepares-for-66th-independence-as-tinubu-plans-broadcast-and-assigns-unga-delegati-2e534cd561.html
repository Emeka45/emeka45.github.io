Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to deliver a nationwide broadcast on October 1 as part of a low-key programme arranged for Nigeria's 66th Independence Anniversary. In tandem with preparations for the national celebration, the Presidency has announced arrangements for international representation at the United Nations.

Preparations for the 66th Independence Anniversary

The Federal Government has inaugurated an inter-ministerial committee to coordinate activities marking the 66th Independence Anniversary. The committee is chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume, who was represented at the inaugural meeting by Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office.

According to the administration, the low-key anniversary programme aims to honour the contributions and legacies of Nigeria's founding figures who fought for independence, while highlighting the administration's reform efforts amidst current socio-economic challenges. Due to time constraints, the committee has been directed to operate with urgency.

The lineup of events includes:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: President Tinubu's Nationwide Broadcast

The planning committee comprises key ministers and national security heads, including the Ministers of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Information and National Orientation, Interior, Foreign Affairs, Federal Capital Territory, and Budget and Economic Planning, alongside the National Security Adviser, Inspector General of Police, Director General of the Department of State Services, and the Commander of the Guards Brigade.

Vice President Shettima Assigned to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a separate directive, President Tinubu mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States. The decision concludes weeks of public speculation regarding the president's attendance following his departure for a three-week European leave in late August.

According to a statement issued by Stanley Nkwocha, Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Communications (Office of the Vice President), Vice President Shettima's delegation includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials.

The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.” Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement addressing global issues tied to national development, peace, security, and economic cooperation, while participating in high-level discussions concerning climate action, sustainable development, and reform of the global financial architecture. He will also engage in bilateral meetings on the sidelines before returning to Nigeria.
