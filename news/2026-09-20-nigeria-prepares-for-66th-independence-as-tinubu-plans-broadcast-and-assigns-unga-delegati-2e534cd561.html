Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is set to address Nigerians on October 1, 2026, as part of a low-key programme arranged for the country's 66th Independence Anniversary. Alongside preparations for the national milestone, the presidency has announced key international assignments, including the delegation tasked with attending the United Nations meetings in New York (Punch).

Low-Key Programme for the 66th Independence Anniversary

To coordinate events leading up to the national day, the Federal Government inaugurated an inter-ministerial committee chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. At the inaugural meeting, Akume was represented by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens (The Reporter).

According to the committee's schedule, celebrations will remain low-key due to time constraints and will feature a structured lineup of events:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: President Tinubu's nationwide broadcast

Akume noted that the anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on the sacrifices of the nation's founding figures while showcasing reform efforts and achievements amidst current socio-economic challenges (The Reporter).

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a related development, President Tinubu mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States (Punch). The decision follows weeks of public discussion regarding whether the president would attend in person following his departure for a three-week European leave on August 30 (Punch).

The Nigerian delegation under Shettima includes state governors such as Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State and Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, alongside ministers and other senior government officials (Punch). The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to September 28, 2026, under the theme "Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all" (Punch).

During the summit, Vice President Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement, outlining national priorities on peace, security, economic growth, and international cooperation, while also participating in high-level discussions concerning climate action, sustainable development, and multilateral partnerships (Punch).
