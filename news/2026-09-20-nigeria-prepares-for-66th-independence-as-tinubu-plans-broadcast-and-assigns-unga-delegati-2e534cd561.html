Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

As Nigeria prepares to mark its 66th Independence Anniversary, President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address the nation in a broadcast on October 1, 2026. The Federal Government has structured the commemoration as a low-level programme, directed by an Inter-Ministerial Committee chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume.

Preparation and Schedule for the 66th Independence Anniversary

Senator George Akume, who was represented at the inaugural committee meeting by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, noted that the anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on the contributions and sacrifices of Nigeria's founding figures. He added that the celebration highlights the administration's reform efforts and achievements amid current socio-economic challenges. Due to time constraints, the SGF instructed the committee to keep the itinerary compact.

The scheduled roadmap for the anniversary includes:

  • September 24, 2026: World Press Conference
  • September 25, 2026: Juma’at Service
  • September 27, 2026: Church Service
  • September 30, 2026: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1, 2026: President Tinubu’s nationwide broadcast

The organizing committee consists of key government figures, including the Ministers of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Information and National Orientation, Interior, Foreign Affairs, Federal Capital Territory, and Budget and Economic Planning, alongside security chiefs and presidential advisers as outlined in reports from The Reporter.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In tandem with domestic anniversary preparations, the Presidency announced that President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of Willoughby United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States. This mandate resolves prior speculation following reports from Nigeria's UN envoy, Jimoh Ibrahim, and follows President Tinubu's departure for a three-week European leave on August 30.

According to a statement issued by Stanley Nkwocha, Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Communications in the Office of the Vice President, the Nigerian delegation features prominent officials, including Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State and Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, alongside ministers and senior personnel.

The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.” During the summit, Vice President Shettima will deliver Nigeria's national statement, addressing matters of national development, peace and security, economic growth, and international cooperation.

Additional engagements for the Vice President in New York include high-level meetings on climate action and the Right to Development, participation in traditional receptions for heads of delegation, and bilateral talks with international leaders and development partners. Further details on these diplomatic itineraries were covered by Punch.
