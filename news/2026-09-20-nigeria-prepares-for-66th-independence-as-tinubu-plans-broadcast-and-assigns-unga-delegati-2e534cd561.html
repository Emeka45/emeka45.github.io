Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

Plans for Nigeria's 66th Independence Anniversary

As the Federal Government prepares to mark Nigeria's 66th Independence anniversary, President Bola Tinubu will deliver a nationwide broadcast on October 1, 2026. The Reporter notes that an Inter-Ministerial Committee has been inaugurated to coordinate the low-key anniversary programme. Chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume—who was represented at the inaugural meeting by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens—the committee has been directed to work with urgency due to limited time.

According to the committee's schedule, events will commence with a World Press Conference on September 24, followed by a Juma'at Service on September 25 and a Church Service on September 27. An Independence Day Public Lecture is also scheduled for September 30 before culminating in the president's broadcast. Senator Akume stated that the anniversary offers an opportunity for reflection on the sacrifices of the nation's founding figures, as well as a chance for the administration to showcase its reforms and achievements amidst ongoing socio-economic challenges.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a related directive, President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States. According to Punch, the decision follows weeks of speculation regarding the president's attendance after he departed Abuja for a three-week European leave on August 30.

Vice President Shettima's delegation includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials. The General Debate runs from September 22 to September 28, 2026, under the theme "Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all." During the assembly, Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement, participate in high-level meetings concerning climate action and the Right to Development, and engage in bilateral meetings to advance economic diplomacy and multilateral cooperation.
