Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address the nation in a broadcast on October 1, 2026, as part of a low-key programme arranged for Nigeria's 66th Independence anniversary (The Reporter). Alongside preparations for the national celebration, the Presidency has announced key international assignments involving Vice President Kashim Shettima.

Low-Key Independence Anniversary Programme

The Federal Government established an Inter-Ministerial Committee to coordinate the low-key anniversary activities, operating under tight timelines. The committee is chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume, who was represented at the inaugural meeting by Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office (The Reporter).

According to the committee's schedule, the lineup of events includes:

  • September 24, 2026: World Press Conference (The Reporter)
  • September 25, 2026: Juma’at Service (The Reporter)
  • September 27, 2026: Church Service (The Reporter)
  • September 30, 2026: Independence Day Public Lecture (The Reporter)
  • October 1, 2026: Nationwide broadcast by President Bola Tinubu (The Reporter)

Senator Akume noted that the anniversary serves to honor the sacrifices of Nigeria's founding figures while allowing the administration to showcase its reform efforts and achievements amidst current socio-economic challenges (The Reporter).

Vice President Shettima Mandated to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a separate directive, President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States (Punch). This decision follows prior speculation regarding the president's attendance after he departed Abuja on August 30 for a three-week vacation in Europe (Punch).

The high-level Nigerian delegation led by Vice President Shettima includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, as well as ministers and senior government officials (Punch).

UNGA General Debate and Strategic Engagements

The 81st UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all” (Punch). During the assembly, Vice President Shettima is expected to:

  • Deliver Nigeria's national statement outlining national priorities on peace, security, economic growth, and international cooperation (Punch).
  • Participate in high-level discussions concerning the Right to Development and climate action (Punch).
  • Engage in bilateral meetings with global leaders, international organizations, and development partners (Punch).

The Presidency stated that these diplomatic engagements aim to advance President Tinubu's economic diplomacy, foster strategic partnerships, and advocate for reforms in the global financial architecture and sustainable development (Punch). Shettima is scheduled to return to Nigeria upon concluding his engagements in New York (Punch).
