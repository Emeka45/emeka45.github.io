Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is set to address Nigerians on October 1, 2026, as part of a low-key programme arranged for the country's 66th Independence Anniversary. Alongside preparing for the national broadcast, the administration has structured celebratory events and assigned leadership for international diplomatic engagements.

Low-Key Independence Anniversary Programme

The Federal Government inaugurated an Inter-Ministerial Committee to coordinate the activities for the 66th Independence Anniversary. Chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume—who was represented at the inaugural meeting by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens—the committee was directed to keep the schedule low-key due to limited time.

According to The Reporter Media, the anniversary provides an opportunity to reflect on the contributions of the nation's founding figures while highlighting ongoing reforms against socio-economic challenges. The event lineup begins with a World Press Conference on September 24, followed by a Juma’at Service on September 25 and a Church Service on September 27. An Independence Day Public Lecture is scheduled for September 30, culminating in President Tinubu's nationwide broadcast on October 1.

The planning committee brings together high-ranking officials, including the Ministers of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Information and National Orientation, Interior, Foreign Affairs, Federal Capital Territory, and Budget and Economic Planning, alongside security chiefs such as the National Security Adviser, Inspector General of Police, and Director General of the Department of State Services.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In a parallel development reported by Punch, President Tinubu mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and lead Nigeria's delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States. This directive closes weeks of speculation regarding the president's attendance following his departure for a three-week European vacation on August 30.

The high-level Nigerian delegation led by Vice President Shettima includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior government officials. The UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.”

During the summit, Shettima is expected to deliver Nigeria's national statement addressing global issues tied to national development, peace and security, economic growth, and international cooperation. His schedule also encompasses high-level meetings concerning climate action, the Right to Development, bilateral discussions with global leaders, and engagements aimed at advancing economic diplomacy and reform of the global financial architecture.
