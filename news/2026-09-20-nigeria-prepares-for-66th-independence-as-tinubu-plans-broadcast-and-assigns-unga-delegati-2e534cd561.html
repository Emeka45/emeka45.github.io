Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme arranged for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, according to The Reporter. Concurrently, the Presidency has directed Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent the President and lead Nigeria's high-level delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, United States, as detailed by Punch.

66th Independence Anniversary Plans

The Federal Government has inaugurated an inter-ministerial committee chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume, to coordinate activities for the 66th Independence anniversary. Represented at the inaugural meeting by the Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, Akume noted that the anniversary offers an opportunity to reflect on the sacrifices of Nigeria's founding figures and to showcase the administration's reform efforts amidst current socio-economic challenges.

Due to time constraints, the SGF directed that the events be kept low-key. The lined-up schedule begins with a World Press Conference on September 24, followed by a Juma’at Service on September 25, a Church Service on September 27, and an Independence Day Public Lecture on September 30. The celebrations will culminate in President Tinubu's nationwide broadcast on October 1.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In international engagements, Vice President Kashim Shettima has been mandated to lead Nigeria's delegation to the 81st UNGA, ending weeks of speculation regarding President Tinubu's attendance following his departure for a three-week European vacation on August 30. Shettima's high-level delegation includes Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, ministers, and other senior officials.

The UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme "Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all." During the summit, Vice President Shettima is expected to:

  • Deliver Nigeria's national statement outlining national priorities on peace, security, and economic growth.
  • Participate in high-level discussions addressing climate action and the Right to Development.
  • Engage in bilateral meetings with world leaders, international organizations, and development partners.
  • Advance the administration's economic diplomacy, focusing on the reform of the global financial architecture and sustainable development finance.

Shettima is scheduled to return to Nigeria upon the conclusion of his engagements in New York.
