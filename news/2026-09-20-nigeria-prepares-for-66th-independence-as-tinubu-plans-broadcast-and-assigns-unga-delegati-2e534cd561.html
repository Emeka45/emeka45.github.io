Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is set to address the nation in a nationwide broadcast on October 1, 2026, as part of a low-key programme marking Nigeria's 66th Independence Anniversary. Alongside the domestic anniversary planning, the presidency announced that Vice President Kashim Shettima has been mandated to represent the president and lead Nigeria's delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, United States.

Low-Key Independence Programme and Committee Setup

The Federal Government inaugurated an Inter-Ministerial Committee to coordinate the low-key anniversary activities, working under strict timelines due to the limited preparation window. The committee is chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume, who was represented at the inaugural meeting by Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office.

According to the SGF, the 66th anniversary provides a platform for citizens to reflect on the sacrifices and legacies of Nigeria's founding figures who fought for independence from colonial rule. Furthermore, the celebration will highlight the administration's reform efforts and achievements amidst current socio-economic challenges.

The approved schedule of events for the anniversary includes:

  • September 24, 2026: World Press Conference
  • September 25, 2026: Juma’at Service
  • September 27, 2026: Church Service
  • September 30, 2026: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1, 2026: President Tinubu's Nationwide Broadcast

The inter-ministerial planning body includes senior government officials, heads of security agencies, and ministers overseeing finance, information, interior, foreign affairs, the Federal Capital Territory, and budget planning. Detailed coverage of the buildup can be followed via The Reporter.

Shettima to Lead Delegation to the 81st UNGA

In tandem with the domestic celebrations, the presidency confirmed that Vice President Kashim Shettima will head the high-level Nigerian team to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York, resolving prior discussions regarding attendance details noted by sources like The Punch.

Shettima's high-level delegation features Governor Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State, Governor Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, along with cabinet ministers and senior government officials. The UNGA General Debate is scheduled from September 22 to 28, 2026, under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.”

During the summit, Vice President Shettima will deliver Nigeria's national statement, outlining national priorities on peace, security, economic growth, and international cooperation. His schedule also encompasses high-level meetings centered on climate action, the Right to Development, traditional receptions for heads of delegation, and bilateral engagements with international partners to advance economic diplomacy and sustainable development goals.
