Nigeria Prepares for 66th Independence as Tinubu Plans Broadcast and Assigns UNGA Delegation to Shettima

President Bola Tinubu is scheduled to address Nigerians on October 1 as part of a low-key programme for the nation's 66th Independence anniversary, alongside directing Vice President Kashim Shettima to lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

President Bola Tinubu is set to address Nigerians on October 1, 2026, as part of a low-key programme arranged for the nation's 66th Independence Anniversary. Concurrently, the presidency has confirmed that Vice President Kashim Shettima will lead the Nigerian delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, United States.

Low-Key Independence Programme and Committee Setup

To coordinate events for the 66th Independence Anniversary, the Federal Government inaugurated an Inter-Ministerial Committee chaired by the Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Senator George Akume. Represented at the inaugural meeting by Bekearedebo Augusta Warrens, Permanent Secretary of the Political and Economic Affairs Office, Akume noted that the anniversary offers a chance to reflect on the legacies of the nation's founding figures while showcasing current reform efforts and administration achievements.

The SGF directed that celebrations remain low-key due to limited time. The structured itinerary includes:

  • September 24: World Press Conference
  • September 25: Juma’at Service
  • September 27: Church Service
  • September 30: Independence Day Public Lecture
  • October 1: Nationwide broadcast by President Bola Tinubu

The planning committee brings together top government figures, including the Ministers of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Information and National Orientation, Interior, Foreign Affairs, Federal Capital Territory, and Budget and Economic Planning, alongside security chiefs such as the National Security Adviser, Inspector General of Police, and Director General of the Department of State Services.

Vice President Shettima to Lead UNGA Delegation

In international engagements, President Tinubu has mandated Vice President Kashim Shettima to represent him and head Nigeria's delegation to the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly in New York. This assignment resolves earlier discussions regarding attendance following the president's departure for a three-week European leave.

Shettima's high-level delegation features Governors Siminalayi Fubara of Rivers State and Dauda Lawal of Zamfara State, alongside ministers and senior government officials. The UNGA General Debate runs from September 22 to September 28, 2026, under the theme "Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all."

During the summit, Vice President Shettima is scheduled to:

  • Deliver Nigeria's national statement detailing national priorities on peace, security, economic growth, and international cooperation.
  • Participate in high-level discussions covering climate action, the Right to Development, and multilateral cooperation.
  • Engage in bilateral meetings with global leaders, international organizations, and development partners to advance economic diplomacy and sustainable development finance.

Following the conclusion of his engagements in New York, Vice President Shettima will return to Nigeria.
