Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State continue to shift ahead of the 2027 general elections, highlighted by the ruling All Progressives Congress expressing strong confidence in its grassroots networks, alongside notable political realignments involving the opposition Peoples Democratic Party in Ogbia Local Government Area, as detailed in reports from The Punch and The Reporter.

APC Grassroots Confidence and Regional Realignment

The state chairman of the APC in Bayelsa State, Warman Weri Ogoriba, stated that the ruling party remains fully confident in its grassroots structures across the state. Ogoriba noted that the party has established solid structures across all eight local government areas and political wards, ensuring that membership and networks are strong enough to prosecute upcoming campaigns. He emphasized that the APC is focused on internal organization, voter mobilization, and continued engagement with stakeholders.

Ogoriba's remarks coincided with broader political developments, including the emergence of the Rainbow Coalition and statements from Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike asserting he holds the political structures required to deliver Rivers and Bayelsa states for President Bola Tinubu in 2027. Reaffirming the party's backing for Tinubu, Ogoriba urged members to remain united and disciplined.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia Local Government Area

Meanwhile, the opposition PDP in Bayelsa recorded a fresh wave of defections as former APC members switched allegiances in Elebele, located within Ogbia Local Government Area. The defectors were led by Gabriel Claudius, popularly known as "One Million Man," and were welcomed during a rally over the weekend.

During the event, Claudius expressed optimism that the PDP is well-positioned to reclaim its political standing in the state and deliver democratic dividends to local communities. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah described the large turnout as a significant morale booster, while criticizing the administration of Governor Douye Diri over economic challenges facing residents despite increased federal allocations. PDP leader Joshua MacIver also dismissed the notion of an easy victory for the APC in 2027, announcing that the party has initiated strategic preparations to contest key legislative seats. As part of his visit to the community, MacIver paid a courtesy call to the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene, and donated twenty million Naira alongside a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace.
