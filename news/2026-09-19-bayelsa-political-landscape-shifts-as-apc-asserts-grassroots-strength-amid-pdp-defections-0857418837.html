Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are witnessing notable realignments ahead of the 2027 general elections. As major political parties position themselves for the upcoming polls, the ruling All Progressives Congress has expressed strong confidence in its grassroots networks, while the opposition Peoples Democratic Party has welcomed fresh defectors in the Ogbia Local Government Area.

APC Asserts Grassroots Strength

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has maintained that its membership and network remain robust enough to prosecute its campaigns successfully. Addressing recent political developments, state party chairman Warman Weri Ogoriba stated that the APC in Bayelsa has established active structures across all eight local government areas and political wards.

Ogoriba emphasized that the party remains focused on internal organization, voter mobilization, and engaging stakeholders across communities. The remarks came against the backdrop of broader political discourse, including comments from Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike regarding political structures in the region, as well as the emergence of the “Rainbow Coalition.” Reaffirming support for President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates, Ogoriba urged members to remain disciplined and united.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia

Meanwhile, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party in the state has recorded defections, welcoming former APC members in Elebele, Ogbia Local Government Area. The group of defectors was led by Gabriel Claudius, popularly known as “One Million Man,” and received during a weekend rally described by party leaders as a major morale booster.

Speaking at the event, Claudius stated that the PDP is prepared to reclaim its political standing and deliver democratic dividends to residents. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah used the platform to criticize the administration of Governor Douye Diri, alleging that economic hardship, poverty, and unemployment have persisted despite increased financial allocations to the state.

Additionally, PDP leader Joshua MacIver dismissed expectations of an easy victory for the APC in 2027, announcing that the opposition has commenced strategic preparations for key legislative contests. During his visit to the area, MacIver also toured the palace of the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI), and announced a donation of ₦20 million alongside a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace, as detailed by The Reporter.
