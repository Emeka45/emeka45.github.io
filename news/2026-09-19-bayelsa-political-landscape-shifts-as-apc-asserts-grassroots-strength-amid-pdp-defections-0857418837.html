Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political realignments in Bayelsa State are gathering momentum ahead of the 2027 general elections, characterized by competing claims of grassroots dominance from the ruling All Progressives Congress and strategic opposition mobilization by the Peoples Democratic Party.

APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid Political Realignments

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has expressed firm confidence in its internal structures, membership networks, and grassroots organization across the state's eight local government areas and political wards. Speaking through the state chairman, Warman Weri Ogoriba, the party emphasized its readiness to prosecute a robust campaign for the upcoming 2027 polls (The Punch).

Ogoriba's remarks came in response to shifting political dynamics, which included the emergence of the “Rainbow Coalition” and comments from Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike asserting his capacity to deliver Rivers and Bayelsa states for President Bola Tinubu in 2027. Reaffirming the party's focus, Ogoriba urged members to remain united, disciplined, and committed to supporting President Tinubu and other party candidates.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia Local Government Area

While the APC maintains confidence in its existing networks, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party has recorded notable defections in Ogbia Local Government Area. A group of former APC members, led by Gabriel Claudius (also known as “One Million Man”), formally defected to the PDP during a rally in Elebele community (The Reporter).

Addressing supporters at the event, Claudius stated that the PDP is well-positioned to reclaim its political standing and deliver democratic dividends to residents. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah welcomed the turnout as a morale booster, while criticizing the administration of Governor Douye Diri over economic hardships and questioning government empowerment initiatives in the area.

Adding to the opposition's strategy, Bayelsa PDP leader Joshua MacIver dismissed expectations of an easy victory for the APC in 2027, noting that the party is undertaking strategic preparations for key legislative contests. During his visit to Elebele, MacIver also paid a courtesy call on the Paramount Ruler, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI), and announced a donation of ₦20 million alongside a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace.
