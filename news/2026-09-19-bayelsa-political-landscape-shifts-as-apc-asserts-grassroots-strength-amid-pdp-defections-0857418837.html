Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, characterized by contrasting assertions of strength from both the ruling All Progressives Congress and the opposition Peoples Democratic Party.

APC Asserts Grassroots Strength

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has expressed firm confidence in its grassroots structures across the state as preparations for the 2027 general elections gather momentum. According to the state chairman of the party, Warman Weri Ogoriba, the party's membership and network remain strong enough to prosecute its campaigns effectively. Speaking in a statement, Ogoriba noted that the APC has established functioning structures across all eight local government areas and political wards, emphasizing that the party's primary focus is on organizational strength and voter mobilization (Punch).

Ogoriba's remarks coincided with political realignments and discussions surrounding the 2027 polls, including claims by the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory, Nyesom Wike, regarding political structures in Rivers and Bayelsa states. Reaffirming the state leadership's commitment to supporting President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates, Ogoriba urged members to remain disciplined and united.

PDP Welcomes Defectors and Criticizes State Government

Meanwhile, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party recorded a fresh wave of defections in Elebele, located within the Ogbia Local Government Area. A group of former APC members, led by Gabriel Claudius—also known as "One Million Man"—switched allegiance to the PDP and were officially received at a weekend rally (The Reporter).

During the event, Claudius stated that the PDP is well-positioned to reclaim its political standing in the state and deliver democratic dividends to local communities. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah described the turnout as a significant morale booster, though he used the platform to criticize Governor Douye Diri's administration over socioeconomic conditions, alleging that poverty and unemployment have worsened despite increased federal allocations to the state.

Another prominent PDP leader, Joshua MacIver, dismissed the likelihood of an easy victory for the APC in 2027, announcing that his party has initiated strategic plans to contest key legislative positions in the National Assembly and the Bayelsa State House of Assembly. Alongside political engagements, MacIver visited the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI), pledging a donation of ₦20 million and a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace.
