Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State continue to experience significant shifts ahead of the 2027 general elections. While the ruling All Progressives Congress expresses firm confidence in its grassroots networks, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party has welcomed a fresh wave of defectors in the Ogbia Local Government Area, according to reports from The Punch and The Reporter.

APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid Realignments

The APC leadership in Bayelsa State has maintained strong confidence in its organizational structures across the state's eight local government areas and political wards. Reacting to recent political realignments, State Chairman Warman Weri Ogoriba emphasized that the party's membership and grassroots networks remain fully equipped to prosecute a strong electoral campaign in 2027.

Ogoriba's remarks coincided with broader discussions surrounding the political landscape, including the emergence of the “Rainbow Coalition” and comments from Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike regarding political structures in the region. Reaffirming the state leadership's commitment to supporting President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates, Ogoriba urged members to remain disciplined, united, and focused on community mobilization.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia Local Government Area

Meanwhile, the opposition PDP in Bayelsa State has countered the ruling party's assertions by welcoming a group of former APC members. Led by Gabriel Claudius, popularly known as “One Million Man,” the defectors crossed over to the PDP during a rally in Elebele, located within the Ogbia Local Government Area.

Speaking to supporters at the event, Claudius stated that the PDP is well-positioned to reclaim its political standing in the state and deliver democratic dividends to local communities. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah described the large turnout as a strong morale booster, though he used the occasion to criticize Governor Douye Diri's administration over economic hardships and transparency regarding state allocations and youth empowerment programs.

Adding to the opposition's strategy, Bayelsa PDP leader Joshua MacIver dismissed expectations of an easy victory for the APC in 2027, announcing that the party has initiated strategic preparations to contest key legislative seats. During his visit to the area, MacIver also paid a courtesy call to the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI), where he announced a donation of ₦20 million and a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new royal palace.

As political parties ramp up preparations and stakeholder engagements across communities, neither the Bayelsa State Government nor the APC leadership had formally responded to the opposition's recent statements at the time of reporting.
