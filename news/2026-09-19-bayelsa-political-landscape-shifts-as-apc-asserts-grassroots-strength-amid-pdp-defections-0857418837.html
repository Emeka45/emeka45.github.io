Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, marked by contrasting developments as the ruling All Progressives Congress expresses strong confidence in its grassroots networks, while the opposition Peoples Democratic Party welcomes defectors in the Ogbia Local Government Area.

APC Reaffirms Grassroots Strength Amid National Realignments

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has expressed firm confidence in its grassroots structures across the state as preparations gather momentum for the 2027 polls. The state chairman of the party, Warman Weri Ogoriba, stated that the party's membership and network remain robust enough to prosecute successful campaigns.

Ogoriba noted that the APC has established operational structures across all eight local government areas and political wards. He emphasized that the party remains focused on internal organization and voter mobilization. These comments followed political realignments, including the emergence of the “Rainbow Coalition” and assertions by Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike regarding his political structures in Rivers and Bayelsa states.

Reaffirming support for President Bola Tinubu and other party candidates, Ogoriba urged members to remain united, disciplined, and committed to advancing the party's collective objectives.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia Local Government Area

Meanwhile, the Peoples Democratic Party in Bayelsa has recorded a fresh wave of defections. Former APC members, led by Gabriel Claudius (popularly known as “One Million Man”), switched allegiance to the opposition party in Elebele, Ogbia Local Government Area.

During a reception rally over the weekend, Claudius stated that the PDP is prepared to reclaim its political standing and deliver democratic dividends. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah described the large turnout as a morale booster. However, Turnah criticized the administration of Governor Douye Diri, alleging that economic hardship, poverty, and unemployment have worsened despite increased state allocations.

Another PDP leader, Joshua MacIver, dismissed suggestions of an easy APC victory in 2027, asserting that the party is organizing strategic contests for key legislative seats. During his visit to the palace of the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI), MacIver also announced a donation of ₦20 million and a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace.
