Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling All Progressives Congress expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition Peoples Democratic Party welcomes defectors in the Ogbia local government area, as reported by Punch and The Reporter.

APC Asserts Grassroots Strength

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has expressed firm confidence in its grassroots network across the state as preparations gather momentum for the 2027 general elections. State party chairman Warman Weri Ogoriba stated that the party's membership and organizational framework remain robust enough to successfully prosecute its campaigns.

Ogoriba noted that the APC has established solid structures across all eight local government areas and political wards. He emphasized that the party's immediate focus is on organizational strengthening and voter mobilization. These comments came amid political realignments, the emergence of the “Rainbow Coalition,” and assertions by Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike regarding political structures in Rivers and Bayelsa states for President Bola Tinubu in 2027.

Ogoriba urged party stakeholders to remain disciplined and committed to supporting candidates at all levels, reaffirming the state leadership's dedication to backing President Tinubu and other party flagbearers.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia

Meanwhile, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party in Bayelsa State has recorded a fresh wave of political defections. Former APC members led by Gabriel Claudius, widely known as “One Million Man,” formally defected to the PDP during a weekend rally held in Elebele, Ogbia Local Government Area.

Speaking to supporters, Claudius stated that the PDP remains fully prepared to reclaim its political standing in the state and deliver democratic dividends to the residents of Elebele and surrounding communities. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah welcomed the turnout as a morale booster, while criticizing the administration of Governor Douye Diri over alleged economic hardships and poverty. Turnah also challenged the state government regarding youth and women empowerment initiatives.

Adding to the discourse, Bayelsa PDP leader Joshua MacIver dismissed notions of an easy APC victory in 2027, announcing that the PDP has initiated strategic preparations to contest key legislative seats. During his visit to Elebele, MacIver also paid a courtesy call on the Paramount Ruler, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI), and donated ₦20 million alongside a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace.
