Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political realignments and grassroots positioning are intensifying in Bayelsa State as major political parties ramp up strategies ahead of the 2027 general elections. While the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) has expressed firm confidence in its grassroots networks, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) has welcomed a fresh wave of defectors from the ruling party (The Punch).

APC Grassroots Confidence Amid Realignments

The APC state chairman, Warman Weri Ogoriba, asserted that the party's membership and organizational network remain robust enough to prosecute a successful campaign in 2027. Speaking on political developments across the state, Ogoriba noted that the party has established solid structures across all eight local government areas and political wards. He emphasized that the APC remains focused on internal organization, voter mobilization, and stakeholder engagement.

Ogoriba's remarks coincided with broader political discourse, including comments from Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike regarding political structures in Rivers and Bayelsa states, as well as the emergence of the Rainbow Coalition. Reaffirming the state leadership's backing of President Bola Tinubu, Ogoriba urged party members to remain disciplined, united, and committed to advancing the party's collective objectives.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia Local Government Area

As the APC underscores its structural reach, the opposition PDP has recorded notable gains at the grassroots. A group of former APC members, led by Gabriel Claudius—popularly known as "One Million Man"—defected to the PDP in Elebele, Ogbia Local Government Area (The Reporter). Welcomed at a weekend rally alongside numerous supporters, Claudius stated that the PDP is well-positioned to reclaim its political standing and deliver democratic dividends to Elebele and surrounding communities.

Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah described the massive turnout at the rally as a significant morale booster. However, Turnah used the platform to criticize the administration of Governor Douye Diri, alleging that poverty, hardship, and unemployment have escalated despite prosperity promises. Turnah challenged the state government to verify empowerment claims through its Centre for Women and Youth Development, arguing that residents continue to face economic hurdles despite higher federal allocations.

Strategic Preparations and Palace Support

Weighing in on the 2027 outlook, Bayelsa PDP leader Joshua MacIver dismissed expectations of an easy victory for the APC, announcing that the party has initiated strategic groundwork to contest key legislative seats in the National Assembly and the Bayelsa State House of Assembly.

During his visit to Ogbia, MacIver also paid a courtesy call on the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI). Expressing concern over the state of the monarch's palace, MacIver announced a donation of ₦20 million alongside a trailer load of cement to support construction work on a new palace facility. Neither the Bayelsa State Government nor the APC leadership had formally responded to the claims made by opposition leaders at the time of the reports.
