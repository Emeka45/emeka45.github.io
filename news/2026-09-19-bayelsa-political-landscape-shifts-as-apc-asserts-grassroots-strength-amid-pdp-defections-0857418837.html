Bayelsa Political Landscape Shifts as APC Asserts Grassroots Strength Amid PDP Defections

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling APC expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition PDP welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

Political dynamics in Bayelsa State are heating up ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) expressing strong confidence in its grassroots structures even as the opposition Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) welcomes defectors in Ogbia local government area.

APC Reaffirms Grassroots Strength

The All Progressives Congress in Bayelsa State has maintained that its membership and network remain robust enough to prosecute successful campaigns across the state. State Chairman Warman Weri Ogoriba emphasized that the party has established solid structures spanning all eight local government areas and political wards. According to Ogoriba, the APC remains focused on internal organization, voter mobilization, and engaging stakeholders as preparations ramp up for 2027.

Ogoriba's remarks came amid evolving political realignments, including discussions surrounding the “Rainbow Coalition” and comments from Federal Capital Territory Minister Nyesom Wike regarding political structures in Rivers and Bayelsa. Reaffirming the state leadership's backing of President Bola Tinubu, Ogoriba urged members to remain united, disciplined, and committed to advancing the party's objectives.

PDP Welcomes Defectors in Ogbia

Meanwhile, the opposition Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) has recorded a fresh wave of defections in Elebele community, Ogbia Local Government Area. A group of former APC members led by Gabriel Claudius, also known as “One Million Man,” formally joined the PDP during a weekend rally.

Addressing supporters, Claudius stated that the PDP is well-positioned to reclaim its political standing in the state and deliver democratic dividends to Elebele and neighboring communities. Bayelsa PDP Chairman George Alabh Turnah hailed the turnout as a significant morale booster. However, Turnah used the platform to criticize Governor Douye Diri's administration, alleging that economic hardship, poverty, and unemployment have worsened. He challenged the state government regarding empowerment metrics and claimed that residents continue to face financial challenges despite increased allocations.

Strategic Preparations and Community Outreach

Bayelsa PDP leader Joshua MacIver dismissed expectations of an easy victory for the APC in 2027, announcing that the opposition has commenced strategic preparations to contest key National Assembly and State House of Assembly seats.

As part of the political engagements surrounding the rally, MacIver paid a courtesy visit to the Paramount Ruler of Elebele, King David Osene Osene (Ogbolo XI). Noting concerns over the condition of the royal palace, MacIver announced a donation of ₦20 million alongside a trailer load of cement to support the construction of a new palace facility.

At the time of reporting, neither the Bayelsa State Government nor the APC leadership had formally responded to the claims and criticisms raised by PDP figures during the Ogbia rally.
