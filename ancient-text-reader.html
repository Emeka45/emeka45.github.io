📜 1 Enoch (Ethiopic Enoch)

An ancient Jewish apocalyptic collection covering the Watchers, Enoch’s journeys, visions of judgment, and a distinctive account of the cosmos. The complete work survives in Ge’ez; this is the English translation by R. H. Charles (1917).

📜 The Book of Jubilees (Little Genesis)

Retells much of Genesis and part of Exodus, arranging sacred history into jubilee periods and emphasizing covenant, law, and a 364-day calendar. This is R. H. Charles’s historic English translation.
