🧭 Research method

A large resource collection is only useful when the information can be checked. Start with a broad reference source, move to scholarly or primary sources, compare dates and jurisdictions, record citations, and distinguish established facts from interpretation. For medical, legal, financial and other high-stakes questions, prefer current authoritative sources.
