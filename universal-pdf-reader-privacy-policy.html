Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Universal PDF Reader handles information. Universal PDF Reader is a PDF reading application developed by C. O. Eric.

Information we collect

Universal PDF Reader is designed to let users read PDF documents on their Android devices. The app does not intentionally collect personal information merely because you open or read a PDF.

Documents selected by the user are handled on the device for reading and related reader functions. We do not intentionally upload the contents of users' personal PDF documents to our own servers.

Files and device storage

The app may request access to files or media when necessary for a user to select, open, or manage PDF documents. Any permissions requested by Android are used for the corresponding app functionality. Users can manage app permissions through Android settings.

Advertising

Universal PDF Reader may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. Advertising providers may collect or use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, diagnostics, and interaction data in accordance with their own policies and applicable law. Advertising preferences and available controls may be managed through Android and the relevant advertising provider.

Third-party services

The app may use third-party services for advertising, distribution, diagnostics, or other legitimate app functions. Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

Children's privacy

Universal PDF Reader is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app's handling of information focused on its intended functionality. No electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, advertising services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions concerning Universal PDF Reader, please use the contact information or public project repository provided by C. O. Eric.
